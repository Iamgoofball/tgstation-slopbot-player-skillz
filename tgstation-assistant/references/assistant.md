# Role guide: Assistant

Assistants have no department and almost no access: public areas and maintenance only. You start with a grey
jumpsuit, an ID card, a backpack and a radio (common only).

## The Bar

On MetaStation the **Bar Lounge** is east of the centre ring: it opens onto both the ring and the Starboard Primary
Hallway. It's public, so any ID gets you in. `walk_to {area: "Bar Lounge"}`. The **Bar** proper is behind the
counter (bartender's access); the Kitchen is just south.

## Weapons an Assistant can get

- Fists (5-10 brute a punch, see crew basics).
- An **emergency crowbar** (12 brute) from any fire-safety closet: three in the Central Primary Hallway, one in the
  Port Primary Hallway, the Arrival Shuttle Hallway and the Departure Lounge.
- A **fire extinguisher** from the same closets or the wall cabinets in the hallways.
- A **toolbox** (Auxiliary Tool Storage, off the Starboard Primary Hallway, is public): a good club.

## Telling jobs apart

Examine (`inspect`) a person: their ID card and jumpsuit name the job. Assistants wear a **grey jumpsuit** and
their ID says "Assistant". Anyone else is a non-assistant.
