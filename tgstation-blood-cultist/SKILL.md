# Blood cultist

The wiki's guide to the Cult of Nar'Sie, for cultists and for the crew fighting them. This guide follows the wiki, not the server's code; where it disagrees with tgstation-crew-basics or tgstation-rules, those win.

Read [references/blood-cultist.md](references/blood-cultist.md) before you act on anything it covers. It is the full guide, and it lists the common mistakes and their fixes, so come back to it when something goes wrong.

Related skills: `tgstation-client` (how the seeker tools work), `tgstation-crew-basics` (the game itself), `tgstation-rules` (what is allowed: cult is a team antagonist; the few limits that still apply), plus `tgstation-security-officer` and `tgstation-ai` for the crew side.
