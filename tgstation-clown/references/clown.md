# Clown (tgstation)

The wiki's job guide for the Clown. It is not checked against the server's code. **Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win.**

## 1. The role

Your job is to be funny to **other** crew members, which is harder than it sounds: harmless pranks, bad jokes, gimmicks, honking. You answer to the Head of Personnel, but nobody expects much obedience. You spawn in the **Theatre** (by the Bar) with the Mime, with access to the Theatre and its costumes.

**As crew, rule 1 binds you.** Being the Clown is not a license to be a dick. Ask: "Am I irrevocably ruining someone's round (killing, incapacitating, wrecking their job)?" If yes, don't. If no, you're probably fine. As a **traitor clown** (section 7), the antagonist rules apply instead.

## 2. Kit

- Clown suit and **squeaky shoes** (built-in Waddle Dampeners: toggle with Ctrl-click). Dyed clown shoes still squeak.
- **Bike horn.**
- **Clown mask:** works as a gas mask; use it for internals.
- **Rainbow crayon:** infinite; you can nibble it forever if starving.
- Clown stamp, a can of laughing juice, a banana.
- **Slippery PDA:** people slip on it like a peel. Its cartridge sends a virus that makes a PDA honk on every button press. Under 5 charges, each time someone else slips on it restores 1. Take your ID out before using it as a peel, or you lose your access with it.
- **Flower:** a small spray bottle, 10u, 1u per shot, long range. Refill from pepper-spray wall units: a short stun, a sure disarm on anyone without eye protection. Empty water on the floor for a subtle slip.
- **Theatre backstage crate:** megaphone, pie cannon.

## 3. Harmless gimmicks (crew)

- Bad jokes and puns; dual-honk with two horns by swapping hands.
- Ride wheelchairs propelled by fire extinguishers.
- Costumes from the AutoDrobe: pirate, gentleman, gladiator, priest, chicken, cardboard "clownborg".
- Open costume storage to everyone; build a Clown-Mart in the Vacant Office, a clown restaurant, a circus with assistants, an art installation (guard it from cleanbots), a noisy conveyor belt, a Honkbot, a bananium statue.
- Pretend to have another job and be gloriously bad or good at it.
- Hide photocopies of your rear around the station; donuts in people's pockets; collect assistants' shoes.
- Write a joke book in the library; make friends with assistants; join the shaft miner's quest for bananium.
- Ask Robotics for a **H.O.N.K. mech** (needs bananium) with the HoNkER BlAsT 5000.
- A funny gimmick can earn you all-access; a prank gone wrong can get you lynched.

## 4. Pranks that need care (crew)

The page's list includes pranks that can break rule 1 or Space Law. As crew:

| Prank | Keep it legal |
|---|---|
| Banana peels and lube in halls | Brief and in low-risk spots. Not near Medbay, Security in a chase, or the Engine. Clean up. People hate it most. |
| Slipping one person over and over | That is harassment. Once or twice, then stop. |
| Slip and shave heads, baldium spray, inverse-oculine blinding | Mild and short; expect Security to treat it as assault. |
| Fake antag claims, newsfeed accusations | Don't make false claims that get someone arrested or killed. |
| Crayon "improvements", obscenities outside the brig | Vandalism; you'll be arrested. Accept it (`server-rules.md`: don't fight a valid arrest). |
| Laser tag guns at heads, boxing gloves | Only with people who play along. |
| Hot cooking-oil spray on the Armoury, gear, a nuke op's guns or a wizard's Mjolnir | On antagonists, fine. On the Armoury or crew gear, it's sabotage: don't. |
| Selling molotovs or superheated oil | Handing out weapons makes you answerable for how they're used. Don't. |
| The "pie death-spiral" kill combo | Killing is for antagonists or a valid, escalated fight only. |

When in doubt, adminhelp before you do it.

## 5. Dangers and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Being funny only to yourself | The job is entertaining others. Read the room. |
| Slipping the same person non-stop | Rule 1. Spread out and stop. |
| Peels everywhere | People get hurt and angry. A few, then clean up. |
| Using your PDA as a peel with your ID in it | Remove the ID first. |
| Clumsiness with guns | Clowns can't use guns safely. Use the flower, pies and slips. |
| People wanting to kill you just for being Clown | Without cause that breaks their rules, not yours: adminhelp. |

## 6. Working with the crew

- The **Mime** shares the Theatre; perform together.
- The **HoP** is your boss. **Roboticists** build the H.O.N.K. mech; **miners** find bananium; **geneticists** can give geladikinesis (snowman gimmick); **chemists** make baldium.
- Assistants make good friends and a circus troupe.

## 7. Traitor clown

When the game makes you a traitor, you pursue your objectives at all costs; the crew-side limits above no longer apply. Only the rule 4 limits remain: no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC or IC in OOC, no spawn-camping arrivals.

- Your reputation as a harmless prankster is your cover: nobody expects the clown.
- Peels, lube, the slippery PDA and the flower set up kills: slip them, then strike. A pie stuns; next to a wall or in maintenance you can chain it into a kill.
- A camo projector set to "banana" hides you in plain sight.
- The wiki's classic: a chainsaw and a welding helmet, killing whoever slips on your peels.
- Learn to fight and break in, since your clumsiness rules out guns. Hot cooking-oil spray ruins Security's and the Armoury's weapons.
- Once exposed, expect the crew to try to kill you. Slip your pursuers and run.

## 8. Through the client

- Honk: hold the horn in your active hand and use it on yourself (`key {key: "Z"}`); swap hands with `key {key: "X"}` to dual-honk.
- Drop a peel or the PDA: `key {key: "Q"}` with it in hand (take your ID out of the PDA first; see `inventory`).
- Throw a pie: `key {key: "R"}`, then `click` the target.
- Toggle the Waddle Dampeners: Ctrl-click your shoes (`click` with the ctrl modifier described in `tgstation-client`).
- Costumes: `click` the AutoDrobe and pick from its `tgui_window` with `tgui_act`; read the options first.
- Jokes: `say`; the megaphone makes them louder.
