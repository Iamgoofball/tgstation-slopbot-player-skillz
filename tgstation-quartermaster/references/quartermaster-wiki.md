# Quartermaster: wiki supplement

Extra advice from the wiki's Quartermaster page, kept only for what `quartermaster.md` doesn't already say. `quartermaster.md` is checked against the server's code; this file isn't. **Where they disagree, `quartermaster.md` wins** (section 6 lists the differences found). Read it as optional background.

## 1. Delegating ("quest giver")

Your meta-job is to give others something to do. Delegate rather than doing everything, and oversee and reward good work.

- Unloading: watch the technicians unload so nothing is stolen. Tear off and stamp the manifests yourself.
- Encourage the crew to do bounties.
- **Downed miners:** a miner on Lavaland who hasn't answered the radio for a few minutes counts as down; send someone to fetch the body (a Paramedic). Tell miners at shift start to carry a **GPS renamed to their own name**.
- **Keep requisition forms** (file them in the cabinet by the console). When the Detective asks who ordered guns, you can answer.
- **Bitrunners:** send someone for upgraded parts from R&D for the quantum server, and **mannitol** from Chemistry for their brain damage.
- Stay active on the Supply channel and always know what your people are doing.

## 2. Ordering judgement

- Few jobs need a crate early, so the opening budget is largely yours to plan.
- Good first orders (wiki): **Insulated Gloves**, **Engineering Equipment** (Engineering Gear), **metal/glass**, **brute medkits**, **standard medkits**.
- Learn what people want: the Roboticist almost always wants metal and glass, the Virologist a Virus crate, the Botanist seeds. Anticipate and have it ready; keep a few insulated gloves and welding masks in stock.
- Handing everything to everyone makes you a security risk. An assistant shouldn't get an RCD; an engineer should. The Clown shouldn't get a combat shotgun; Security may.
- Listen on Command and react: rumours of a cult, buy **holy water**; armory spaced, buy the HoS new guns (both with the Captain's OK, per `quartermaster.md`).
- **Revolution:** you can order **mindshield implants** for Security en masse; revolutionaries will try to take the bay to stop you and get weapons. Watch your back.

## 3. Autolathe and MULEbots

- The autolathe starts empty. Auxiliary Tool Storage's metal and glass keep it running a long time.
- Hacking the autolathe unlocks items like RCDs and flamethrowers: cut and mend wires until the blue light goes off; wear insulated gloves (the green light means it shocks). Hacking is not a job duty; Security tolerates a little, but don't build walls or fortify Cargo with an RCD.
- Many QMs hack MULEbots to carry non-standard cargo and run faster; a better power cell also helps.

## 4. Contraband

- **Contraband posters/crates:** take the supply console's circuit board out, multitool it to change its receiver spectrum, reinstall it. Contraband is illegal: Security may confiscate it and arrest you for it. Don't do this as crew without a reason the rules allow.
- Emagging a supply console unlocks the **Spec Ops crate** (grenades, a sleepy pen, more). See section 7.

## 5. Small tips

- Riding a MULEbot protects you from facehuggers.
- Right-click a cap to flip it backwards.
- Projectiles, bomb damage (including the miners' proto-kinetic accelerators), emitters and gibtonite slowly break crate locks. Breaking crates you shouldn't open is theft; see `quartermaster.md` on locked crates (take them to the owner).
- **Package wrapper** lets you wrap things that wouldn't otherwise fit in a backpack (space suits).
- At shift start, check the Mining Office: the **boulder retrieval machines** need the conveyor switched on **and** the retrieval machine turned on (right-click). Every station starts with one Lavaland vent sending small iron and glass boulders, even with no miners.

## 7. Rules notes

- **If you are an antagonist:** you have easy access to weapons. Order a weapons crate and hide it (or send the crate back and stash the loot), arm the assistants to do your objectives, emag a console for the Spec Ops crate (the one in your office points straight at you; the bay one points at Cargo), open crates with an emag, emitters or gibtonite, emag the MULE for a distraction and blame a technician. Move cash to your ID to Buy Privately. Antagonists may pursue their objectives by any means; `tgstation-rules` still forbids metagaming and metacomms, bug or exploit abuse, erotic or creepy behaviour, OOC in IC, and spawn-camping arrivals. Once exposed, expect the crew to try to kill you.
- **Confirmed antagonists:** once you are certain beyond doubt that someone is an antagonist, lethal force is fine and expected (most can't be held safely, and dead antagonists return to the ghost pool). Confirm first.
