# Wizard (tgstation)

Rewritten from the wiki's Wizard page. It is not checked against the server's code, so numbers may have drifted. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win. Section 9 lists the rule limits that still apply to you.

## 1. The role

The Wizard is a lone antagonist sent by the Space Wizard Federation. You start on the Wizard's Den, an unreachable ship, spend 10 points on spells and artifacts, then teleport to the station and do what your objectives (or the Grand Ritual) ask. Difficulty: very hard. Once spotted, the whole station is told and Security comes for you. You are a lone antagonist under rule 4 of `server-rules.md`: any goal, but no metagaming, exploit abuse, OOC in IC or spawn-camping arrivals.

## 2. Kit and the Den

- **You start with:** wizard robes and hat (worn), a spellbook in your backpack, and a **four-use Scroll of Teleport**. If you don't buy the Teleport spell, the scroll is your only way to the station. You can't use the Teleport spell to get to the station. You need a free hand to use the scroll.
- **No flashlight.** Maintenance is pitch black.
- **Once you leave the Den you can't come back.** Prepare there.
- **Magic mirror** (bedroom): change name, appearance and species. The AI treats a wizard as human unless you change species (or become a skeleton via Bind Soul).
- **Advanced camera console**: watch the station before leaving, pick a quiet landing spot.
- **In the Den you can learn and refund spells as often as you like.** Test them there.
- Staffs and brooms lying around the Den are decoration or weak melee weapons.
- Staves, swords and hammers don't fit in bags; wear them on your back.

**Robes and hat matter.** Roughly half the spells (the "robed" table) need both worn. Lose them and those spells won't cast. Few wizards carry a spare hat; consider one in your bag. Exceptions: the Mastercrafted Armor Set counts as robes; Chuuni Invocations replaces the hat with an eyepatch that counts.

## 3. Quick start checklist (in the Den)

1. Mirror: name, species, look.
2. Spend 10 points in the spellbook (sections: Deadly, Defensive, Magical Transportation, Assistance and Summoning; plus Loadouts, Perks, Rituals, Arcane Randomizer).
3. Wear a robe and a hat (unless you plan a stealth or robeless build).
4. Optional: grab anything useful from the Den.
5. Optional: scout a landing site on the camera console.
6. Use the scroll (free hand) to teleport in.

Cast spells by clicking their action buttons (top left). Red means on cooldown. Spells can be upgraded after learning, which shortens cooldowns (listed below as level 1 / ... / level 5) or strengthens them.

## 6. Rituals and the Grand Ritual

**Rituals** are one-time spells cast from the book on your ship:

| Ritual | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Curse of Madness | 4 | Gives the whole station lasting brain traumas, with your message. 20% are magical traumas: Athaumasia (immune to magic), Lumiphobia (burned by light), Poltergeist (objects thrown at them), Stalking Phantom (an invisible stalker that mutilates). |
| Summon Ghosts | 0 | Crew see ghosts. Ghosts will reveal where you are. |
| Summon Guns | 2 | A gun at everyone's feet (not you or apprentices); some crew become survival antagonists. Dynamic: maximum threat only. |
| Summon Magic | 2 | Magic trinkets for everyone; some crew become antagonists collecting magic. Dynamic: maximum threat only. |
| Summon Events | 2 | Replaces random events with wizard events (cursed items, name swaps, lava floors, RPG loot...); repeat casts raise the rate, level 5 is one a minute. Dynamic: maximum threat only. |
| Mass Wizard Teaching | 2 + the spell's cost | Teaches everyone one spell or item, made robeless; you get it too. Dynamic: maximum threat only. |

The RPG Loot event gives every item an upgrade level from -15 to +15 and an affix, plus fortification scrolls in about 25% of containers (+1 each; above +9 each step has a growing chance to backfire to a quarter). Notable effects: batons +4 stamina damage per level (stamcrit in one hit at +12), gloves +10% shock resistance per level (insulated at +10), storage gains slots and size, slippery items stun at +5 and beat galoshes at +10, shoes and armour get faster. Rare affixes include Vampiric (heals 10 per hit), Soul-Stealing, Thunderfury (tesla on hit), Shrapnel (fires projectiles), Curse of Hunger (undroppable), Curse of Polymorph (transforms whoever picks it up).

**The Grand Ritual** is an alternate objective every wizard has. Your objectives panel names seven locations in turn.

1. Away from the spot, the Grand Ritual action button points you toward it.
2. On the spot, the button draws a rune, clearing whatever covers it (including any plasma tanks nearby, explosively).
3. Click the rune: three steps, each ending in part of an incantation. Each completed ritual makes the next one longer (up to the 7th). You're helpless while casting, but you can stop and resume from the last finished step.
4. Each ritual points you to the next place, triggers a station event that grows more severe (the sixth can bring a space dragon), and a random local effect (lube, gold, a summoned crew member).
5. **From the fourth ritual on, each new circle's location is announced on the radio.**
6. The seventh is the finale, an extended ritual with a chosen effect: **Armageddon** (90 minutes into the round; kills you; singularity, tesla ball or endless meteors), **Connection** (all doors unbolted and access removed), **Evolution** (Summon Magic), **Jubilation** (the station becomes a clown station; some crew get an objective to kill you), **Transformation** (the station slowly turns into gold, wood, meat or pizza), **Usurpation** (you become the legal Captain with all access; real Captains become Assistants in maintenance; some crew get an objective to kill you), **Continuation** (a random event). You may keep going after the seventh for more events.

## 7. Loadouts and perks

Ten points. Spellbook presets ("Wizard Approved Loadouts"):

- **Classic:** Fireball, Magic Missile, Smite, Ethereal Jaunt x2. Easy to use.
- **Mjolnir's Power:** Mjolnir, Instant Summons, Mutate, Forcewall, Blink, Tesla Blast.
- **Fantastical Army:** Soulstone kit, Staff of Change, Necromantic Stone, Teleport, Ethereal Jaunt. No attack spells; minions fight.
- **Soul Trapper:** Soul Tap, Smite, Flesh to Stone, Mind Swap, Knock, Teleport. Mind Swap into new bodies to reset Soul Tap's max-health cost.

Popular community builds:

- **Survivalist:** Fireball or Mutate, Blink, Ethereal Jaunt, Warp Whistle, The Traps, 2 spare (Wand Assortment + Charge for on-demand healing).
- **Stealth:** Fireball, Knock, Wild Shapeshift (mouse to ventcrawl), Untie Shoes level 4, 4 spare for robeless spells. Pick a believable name, get crew clothes fast, ask for a new ID; carry robes in your bag.
- **Wizard Gang:** 2 apprentice contracts, Guardian Deck, Frog Contract, Soulstone kit.
- **Cancermancer:** Flesh to Stone, Mind Swap, Staff of Animation, Fireball, Bottle of Tickles x2. Petrify, animate, swap into the statue.
- **Murderboner:** Fireball (or Mjolnir + Instant Summons), Time Stop, Ethereal Jaunt or Rod Form, The Traps, 2-3 spare. All offence, little defence: kill before you are killed.
- **Space Asshole:** Summon Events x5 (an event every minute); watch the chaos from the Den's camera console. Expect admin attention and a crew that hates you.

**Arcane Randomizer** (not refundable): Semi-Randomize gives 12 points of spells with some mobility and lethality; Full Random gives 15 points of anything.

**Perks** (passive): Déjà vu (every 60 s you return to where and how healthy you were 60 s ago), Four Hands, Gamble (2 random perks), Heart Eater (eat hearts for resistance, a mutation and strong healing), Magnetism (an orbiting gravity anomaly pulls things to you), Slime Friends (an evil slime ally every 15 s, costs nutrition), Spells Lottery (a chance at a free purchase, but no more refunds), Transparence (projectiles pass through you; -25% health; a curse hunts you), Worm Born (reborn once as a big worm; blocks Bind Soul).

## 8. Tactics

- **Once exposed, expect the crew to try to kill you.** Confirmed antagonists are fair game for lethal force; plan your escape before you are seen.
- Arrive and grab protection fast: **sunglasses** (shrug off Asimov cyborgs' flashes) and **insulated gloves** (for when the AI turns on you).
- You can cast most spells while knocked down. Jaunt then makes you invulnerable but you can't move until the stun ends; touch spells still need you to hit.
- Disable Tech empties batons, flashes and energy guns and stops cyborgs, but shocks doors: avoid doors right after.
- Repulse buys time for an escape spell to come back.
- Staff of Animation on knives, hatchets and other strong melee items makes the deadliest minions.
- Mind Swap when losing a one-on-one melee with nobody else near; both of you are out for a while.
- Bind Soul: hide the phylactery somewhere remote, inside another container; move it between revivals.
- Instant Summons any weapon you rely on (a staff, a fire axe), so a thief can't keep it.
- The Scrying Orb lets you hear deadchat. **Don't use what ghosts say there**: that is metagaming (rule 2 of `server-rules.md`).
- Summon Ghosts ruins stealth: ghosts can tell the crew where you are.
- Curse of Madness messages: nobody is obliged to obey a command in them.
- Reliable damage: Fireball, Mutate, Mjolnir. Smite is a slow melee instant kill; Lightning Bolt trades damage for area hits; Spellblade takes limbs; Singularity Hammer pulls instead of pushes; Spell Cards is weak.
- Gimmick (non-lethal) options: Staff of the Locker, Barnyard Curse, Staff of Change.

## 9. Dangers, common mistakes and rule conflicts

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Losing the hat or robes (acid, fire smoke grenades, stripping) | Carry a spare hat; build some robeless spells; never let anyone strip you. |
| Muzzled, can't cast | Most spells need you to speak; get the muzzle off, use robeless non-verbal ones. |
| Arriving in dark maintenance with no light | Bring or find a flashlight; scout the landing on the camera console. |
| Leaving the Den unprepared | You can't return. Test and refund spells first. |
| Fireballing at close range | It hurts you; throw it far. |
| Bees, demons and simians turning on you | They attack you too. Have an escape ready or the MODsuit. |
| A crew member grabs your staff or wands | Instant Summons them back; Staff of Change and others backfire on non-wizards only partly. |
| Guardian plus Mind Swap | Guardian damage follows your body; don't mix them. |

**Limits that still apply** (`server-rules.md` section 4, lone antagonists): no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC or IC in OOC, no spawn-camping arrivals. Everything else (killing, destruction, the Grand Finale of your choice) is yours to use for your objectives.

- Scrying Orb deadchat and ghost tips are OOC information; acting on them is metagaming.

## 10. For the crew: fighting a wizard

- Strip the hat or robes (acid or fire smoke grenades work): about half the spells stop.
- Muzzle a restrained wizard to stop most casting.
- Anyone under 50% health can be knocked out with a briefcase; wizards do the same to you.
- Wizard Knock opens bolted doors; don't trust a door to hold.
- Pick up dropped staffs and wands only if you must; several backfire on non-wizards.
- Suicide or welder bombing a wizard is risky: the page warns that missing can get you banned.
- A wizard in robes casting spells is a confirmed antagonist: lethal force is fine. Most can't be held in a cell (Jaunt, Knock, Teleport), so kill rather than brig, and strip the hat and robes off the body.

## 11. Through the client

- **Spellbook and other windows:** click the spellbook with it in hand to open it; `tgui_window` reads the entries and their refs, `tgui_act` buys or refunds. Re-read after each purchase to see your points.
- **Casting:** spells are action buttons on the HUD. `screen` lists them; `click` the button. Aimed spells then need a `click` on the target or tile. Check `status` for cooldowns rather than spamming.
- **Mirror and scroll:** `click` the mirror, answer its prompts (`answer_prompt`, `answer_alert`). Use the scroll in hand (`click` it while it is your active-hand item) and pick the area from the list.
- **Grand Ritual:** its action button gives the direction in chat; `walk_to` the named area, press the button again, then `click` the rune and `wait` through each step.
- **Talking:** incantations are spoken automatically. Use `say` sparingly; `;` reaches Common.
- **Fighting:** `fight {target, goal}` for melee with a staff or hammer; spells by button.
- Keep a hand free before the scroll and before two-handed spells (`inventory` shows your hands).
