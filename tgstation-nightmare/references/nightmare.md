# Nightmare (tgstation)

Rewritten from the wiki's Nightmare page. It is not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win.

## 1. The role

A Nightmare is a side antagonist from a random event (or alongside changelings): a shadow creature that heals in darkness and dies in light. A glass cannon: your armblade kills fast, but one stun in the light can finish you. You have no access and no ID. Difficulty: hard. You are a lone antagonist (`server-rules.md` section 4).

## 2. Abilities

| Ability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Light Eater** (armblade, always on you) | Works as an armblade. Hitting a light source turns it to dust; hitting a person destroys every **lit** light they carry. Permanently destroys a cyborg's headlamp. |
| **Shadow Walk** | In enough darkness, jaunt freely through the shadows; pop out, strike, pop back. Moving into light throws you out of the jaunt. |
| **Darkness** | You heal in darkness and take damage in light. |
| **Night vision** | You see in the dark. |
| **Dodge** | In darkness you dodge every ranged projectile. |

## 3. Organs (if a Nightmare dies)

| Organ | Implanted effect |
|---|---|
| Tumorous Mass (brain) | Host gains Shadow Walk and becomes a Nightmare controlled by the original Nightmare. |
| Heart of Darkness | Host's arm becomes a permanent light eater; revives the host from death in darkness. The host stays crew, **not** an antagonist. |
| Burning red eyes | Host sees perfectly in darkness. |

## 4. Tactics (as the Nightmare)

- Stay in maintenance and dark rooms; break lights to extend the dark.
- Ambush loners: one hit eats their lights, then they can't see you.
- Pick fights where you can escape into darkness; pull out the moment you take a stun in light.
- Eat cyborg headlamps first.
- **Once exposed, expect the crew to try to kill you.** Confirmed antagonists are fair game for lethal force; keep a dark retreat at hand.
- Kill quickly and vanish; you are a lone antagonist, so only the limits in `server-rules.md` section 4 bind you (no metagaming, exploits, OOC in IC, spawn-camping).

## 5. For the crew: surviving a Nightmare

- **Stay in light.** It won't bother you in a lit room with lit rooms around. Departments like Security or the Bridge are safest. If lights start breaking, move.
- **Bring a friend.** Alone, one hit kills your lights and you're dead. A stunbaton hit in the dark just makes it jaunt away until the stun wears off. Two people force it to hit both, and one can pull the other out.
- **Bring a spare light, turned off.** The light eater only destroys lights that are on. A glowstick or flashlight that isn't lit survives; switch to it after you're hit.
- **Deny escape.** In the dark it just leaves when losing. Crack a glowstick and throw it to block its retreat, and stay close.
- A shadow creature with an armblade is a confirmed antagonist: kill it. It can't be held in a cell (it jaunts through darkness). Being afraid of the dark doesn't justify attacking an ordinary crew member you only suspect.

## 6. Through the client

- The armblade is in your hand: `click` a light, lamp or person to strike it; `fight {target, goal}` for a full fight.
- Shadow Walk is an action button (`screen` lists it); `click` it in darkness, then `move` or `walk_to`. `look` shows how lit your tiles are only indirectly, so expect to be kicked out near lights.
- As crew: keep a glowstick or flashlight off in your bag (`inventory`); to deny escape, light it in hand (`click` it) and throw it (`R`, then `click` the tile).

## 7. Checklist
