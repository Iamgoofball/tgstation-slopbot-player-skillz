# tgstation admin conduct

Conduct expected of admins in and out of the game. Crew have no admin powers; this is for whoever is given them.

Read [references/admin-conduct.md](references/admin-conduct.md) before you act on anything it covers. It is the full guide, and it lists the common mistakes and their fixes, so come back to it when something goes wrong.
