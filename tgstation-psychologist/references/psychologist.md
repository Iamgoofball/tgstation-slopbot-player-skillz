# Psychologist (tgstation)

Rewritten from the wiki's Psychologist page. It is not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win.

## 1. The job

You are the crew's listening ear. Nominally Service, physically in medbay, you defuse disputes and look after the crew's mood and mental health. Superiors: Head of Personnel and Chief Medical Officer. Difficulty: easy. You aren't vital to the station, but you can make it run better. **Bare minimum: talk to any crew member who asks for you.**

## 2. Kit, access and radio

- **Access:** Medbay, Psychology Office; extra: Coroner's office.
- **Worn/carried:** black suit or suit skirt, laceup shoes, clipboard, medical PDA, a few pills.
- **Headset:** Medical (`:m`) and Service (`:v`).
- **In the office:** medical records laptop, carbon paper, filing cabinet, couch and chair, a moth plushie, a straitjacket, muzzle, earmuffs and blindfold.

## 3. Duties

- **Talk and listen** to anyone sad, angry, confused, traumatised or bored.
- **Prescriptions and doctor's notes.** Write them on paper, sign them, hand them out. Patients redeem them with the CMO, Chemists, Doctors, or (for some) the Botanist or Bartender.
- **Mediate.** Work with the HoP, CMO, other heads and Security to talk disputes down.
- **Rehabilitate prisoners** and help them back into work.
- **Lawyers:** collaborate on injustices; act as an expert witness at trials.
- **Medical records:** keep them up to date on the console or laptop.
- Optional: bar roleplay; with surgery access, lobotomies on volunteers; studying the clown. Talking down antagonists is usually fatal.
- The page links a code change saying psychologists can **slow supermatter delaminations** (by being near the crystal, presumably calming it). Not checked; don't go near the crystal without Engineering.

## 4. Mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sitting in the office unseen | Announce on Common that you're open; visit the brig and bar. |
| Using the straitjacket or muzzle on someone without cause | That's assault. Only with the patient's consent, or for Security/Medical on a real danger. |
| "Social experiments" that harm people | Non-antagonists need IC reasons and must not grief (rule 1). Keep experiments harmless. |
| Unsigned prescriptions | Sign every one; chemists won't honour them otherwise. |
| Negotiating with an armed antagonist alone | Leave it to Security. |

## 5. As an antagonist

You have a private room few can enter, ties to two heads, and a harmless-looking job people trust. The wiki's suggestion: buy a **hypnotic flash** from your uplink and use it on patients. Follow `tgstation-rules` for antagonist limits.

## 6. Through the client

- Writing: hold a pen in your active hand and `click` a paper; type into the window (`tgui_window` / `tgui_act`), sign at the end. Carbon paper makes a copy.
- Records: `click` the laptop, read with `tgui_window`, edit with `tgui_act`.
- Sessions: `say` in person; `me` for gestures; `read_chat` to follow up. Calls for you come on Medical `:m` or Service `:v`.

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Office checked: pills, paper, pen, records laptop.
- [ ] Common: "Psychology Office open in medbay."
- [ ] Each patient: listen, note in records, prescription or note if needed.
- [ ] Visit the brig's prisoners; offer to testify for the Lawyer.
