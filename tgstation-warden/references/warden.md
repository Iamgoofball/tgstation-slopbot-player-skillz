# Warden (tgstation)

Derived from the wiki's Warden page; not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win. Arrest procedure, the brig timer and record screens, and Space Law sentences are in `tgstation-security-officer`; this guide covers what is specific to the Warden.

**Confirmed antagonists.** Once someone is determined beyond doubt to be an antagonist, Security may kill them. Most antagonists can't be safely held in a cell, and a dead antagonist returns to the ghost poll for midround antagonists and events, so this is normal and expected. Confirm first; suspects and ordinary criminals get non-lethal arrest and Space Law. Don't fill your cells with confirmed antagonists who will break out.

## 1. The role in one paragraph

You run the Brig, the Prison Wing and the prisoners, and you guard the Armory. Half the job is keeping prisoners alive, fairly sentenced and inside; the other half is arming Security when war comes. You are the Head of Security's right hand and take command of Security if the HoS is gone. You are not a patrol officer: **stay in the Brig, and watch the Armory.** Difficulty: hard. Minimum: nobody who shouldn't be is in the Armory, every arrested person has a timer, and nobody breaks out. By default the Warden can't be an antagonist.

## 2. Kit, access and radio

- **Access:** Armory, Security, Brig, holding cells, Courtroom, weapon permit; Maintenance, Morgue and the Detective's Office as extras.
- **Radio:** Security (`:s`).
- **Locker:** brig door remote, a box of flashbangs, extra zip ties, and burn-resistant Kaza Ruk gloves (§8). Bag some of it. Also take a laser: if the APC is blown, the Armory windoors lose power and you can't get through them to stop a looter.
- **Armory:** barricades, portable flashers, a toolbox to install flashers, deployable barrier grenades, implants, the Security Door Control remote (bolts chosen Brig doors), plus anything printed at the Security protolathe or confiscated from antagonists.
- **Your ID is a high-value target.** Don't lose it. Know who the real HoS is, in case someone kills them and takes their identity.

## 3. Where things are

Your domain is the Brig, Security Office, Prison Wing and your office (the Warden's Office); the Courtroom is next door. Your office has the security records console, the crew monitoring console, camera access, the Prisoner Management Console and the lockdown button grid. Most stations have an external camera in space behind the Armory.

## 4. Core procedures

- **Timers.** Every prisoner gets a cell time that fits Space Law. Release them when it ends.
- **Dispatch.** With no prisoners, coordinate officers on the Security radio. Follow up on accusations heard on the radio; fill in arrest records on the security records console so officers know why someone is wanted; check cameras for reported disturbances and update the records of those involved. Watch officers' positions and health on the crew monitor.
- **Sweeps.** Every few minutes, check the cells for hurt prisoners or break-ins, and check the Armory; it can be opened quietly. Stun anyone suspicious in the Brig or Security Office and hold them for the HoS. With suspects, **disarm first; kill only if it is the only option.** Anyone inside the Armory without express permission from the Captain, Warden or HoS may be shot on sight: there is almost never a good reason to be there otherwise. The exception is an emergency where Security is handing out guns. A confirmed antagonist may be killed anywhere.
- **Evidence.** Almost anyone with Brig access (even lawyers) can open evidence storage, so put dangerous evidence in the right locker in the Armory. Help the Detective with timers and evidence when they bring someone in.
- **Confiscated gear.** Keep antagonist weapons and items away from officers; only you or the HoS handle them.
- **Mindshields.** All Security start implanted. Check every officer and head with your Security HUD; someone without one may be an impostor (changeling, traitor).

## 5. Implants

The Prisoner Management Console looks up and can neutralize most implants.

| Implant | Effect |
|---|---|
| Chemical | Load up to 50 u by injecting a filled syringe into it while still cased; inject remotely from the console. Can be sedative or lethal; implant several times for larger doses. Malnutrition may make it fire early or break. Gone 10 min after death unless revived. Blue dot on a SecHUD. |
| Tracking | Location and mind messages from the console; track with a bluespace locator (Security techfab, after research). Gone 10 min after death unless revived. Red dot on a SecHUD. |
| Mindshield | De-converts revolutionaries; whoever resists it is likely a head rev. Blocks brainwashing by revs and cultists but de-converts only revs. More from Cargo at very high cost. |
| Beacon | Teleport to the person with a teleporter or hand teleporter. The console won't give location, only whether they are somewhere dangerous like space. |
| Bluespace grounding | Blocks all teleporting (heretics escaping, kidnaps); an attempt stuns them for several seconds. |
| Exile | Can't return through the Gateway or use Lavaland shuttle consoles. |

## 6. Lockdown

The button grid in your office drops shutters and blast doors on the Brig front, the front desk, the Perma wing and the Armory. Use it when a mob comes for the Brig. Deployable barrier grenades from the Armory seal areas quickly and are repaired with a welder.

## 7. Prison Wing and labor camp

- **Prison Wing (perma):** no timers, for the most heinous crimes only (many antagonists are killed before it comes to this), and only you, the HoS or the Captain take people there. Remove tools, weapons and contraband, but **don't remove prisoners' backpacks** (the page calls that griefing). Check it often so officers, carp, antagonists or meteors don't make it a death camp.
- **Roundstart prisoners** are non-violent and fairly trustworthy but will escape if they can. Keep good relations (fulfil requests, chat), and punish bad behavior.
- **Labor camp:** for unruly prisoners. Give them a prisoner ID **with a point goal set**, or they may be stuck forever, and show them the card console on the labor shuttle. They get a small medbay, toilets and mining gear. Officers have died to the labor camp pickaxe; be careful.

## 8. Kaza Ruk gloves

- Shoves may disarm the target's item into your hand.
- 50% chance of a follow-up bare-hand hit (a third of base damage, higher chance to break bones).
- Three action buttons; pressing one makes your next attack that move:
  - **Low Sweep:** instant knockdown plus some stamina damage. Follow with a shove or baton.
  - **Lung Punch:** heavy oxygen damage; a few can knock someone out for about 5 minutes.
  - **Neck Chop:** mutes briefly. Mute and stun a wizard, then cuff, strip the robes and muzzle them (they can still use wands and melee).

## 9. Dangers and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Leaving the Brig to patrol | Stay in. Dispatch officers instead. |
| Armory broken into from space | Watch the external camera; if you hear tools, suit up (Security hardsuit) and go. |
| Officers killed just outside the Brig doors | Watch for loiterers outside; something may be being set up. |
| Prisoner never released | Set and track timers. |
| Labor camp prisoner stuck forever | Always set the point goal. |
| HoP or a clown demanding Armory gear | The HoP is not your superior or in Security. The HoS alone decides who joins Security; check with them. An HoP breaking into the Armory unauthorized goes in a cell for the HoS or Captain (and may be an impostor). |
| Beating human prisoners on camera | The Asimov AI acts on human harm. Don't harm ordinary prisoners in the open, and don't mistreat crew criminals beyond Space Law. (The page's joke that non-humans may be beaten freely is not guidance for crew prisoners.) |
| Holding a confirmed antagonist who escapes | Most can't be contained; kill confirmed antagonists. |

**If you are an antagonist.** By default the Warden can't be one; if the game makes you one anyway, the page's "Warden from Hell" tactics apply: you start with gloves, an energy gun, armor and sunglasses, and own the Armory, Brig and gulag. Station access is poor and roaming draws attention, so act fast and return to the Brig. Take steal targets from whoever was caught with them; for assassinations, most people pass through the Brig: stun, strip and space them, and say their time was up. If Security gets onto you, a CO2 canister from the execution chamber hooked into the Brig air supply kills everyone there. The limits in `server-rules.md` rule 4 still apply (no metagaming, metacomms, exploits, erotic or creepy behaviour, OOC in IC, or spawn-camping arrivals).

## 10. Working with others

- **HoS:** your superior. You command Security only if they're gone.
- **Officers:** not under your command outside the Brig, but you may order them out if they muck around inside it. Be the eye in the sky.
- **Detective:** let them work; help with timers and evidence.
- **Lawyers:** useful as extra eyes; give spare sunglasses or a SecHUD; don't deny clients counsel; criminal lawyers get arrested like anyone.

## 11. Through the client

- `walk_to {area: "Warden's Office"}` (name from the map) at shift start, then stay put; `look` to watch the cell block.
- Dispatch with `say {text, radio: "security"}`; `wait` with `until` on a word to catch reports.
- Records console, Prisoner Management Console, crew monitor, cameras and brig timers are tgui windows: open with `click`, read actions with `tgui_window`, act with `tgui_act`. Brig timer and records actions are in `tgstation-security-officer` §4.
- Kaza Ruk moves are action buttons on your HUD; see `screen` for them, then `click` the button before attacking.
- Lockers: click once to unlock, again to open.

## 12. Checklist

- [ ] Check every officer and head for a mindshield.
- [ ] Timer set for every prisoner; release on time.
