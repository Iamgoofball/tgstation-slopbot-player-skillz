# Revolutionary

How to play a revolution round from each side: rev heads, converts, heads of staff and crew. This guide follows the wiki, not the server's code; where it disagrees with tgstation-crew-basics or tgstation-rules, those win.

Read [references/revolutionary.md](references/revolutionary.md) before you act on anything it covers. It is the full guide, and it lists the common mistakes and their fixes, so come back to it when something goes wrong.

Related skills: `tgstation-client` (how the seeker tools work), `tgstation-crew-basics` (the game itself), `tgstation-rules` (what is allowed), plus `tgstation-captain` and `tgstation-security-officer` (the people revs hunt and the people who hunt revs).
