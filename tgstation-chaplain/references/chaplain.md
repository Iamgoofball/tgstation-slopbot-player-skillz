# Chaplain (tgstation)

Derived from the wiki's Chaplain page; not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win.

## 1. The role in one paragraph

You are the station's clergy, in Service under the Head of Personnel. You hold funerals and services, counsel and hear confessions, preach a religion of your own invention, cremate and space-bury the dead, and, when a cult appears, become one of Security's most important allies: your holy water de-converts cultists and your null rod makes you immune to cult magic. Difficulty: easy. Minimum: hold a funeral when asked, cooperate with Security against a cult, follow your religion. Call people to the Chapel on the radio; otherwise nobody comes.

## 2. Kit, access and radio

- **Access:** Chapel office, Morgue, Crematorium, Theatre. Nothing else.
- **Radio:** Service and Common.
- **Holy book**, **null rod**, **armaments beacon**, a black "Mysterious Old Book of Cloud-Chasing" (teaches one person the Smoke spell), candles and crayons in your locker, coffins and burial garments, a confession booth.
- **Keep the holy book and null rod on you at all times.**

## 3. The holy book

- Set your god's and religion's names in Character Setup **before** the round, or you get the default. The first use in a round lets you pick its skin (Quran, white Bible, clown bible, Necronomicon and more).
- Starts with a bottle of whiskey and maybe junk. It holds only **one small item**, and fits in your hoodie's suit storage. Worn in the exosuit slot it blocks 1 bullet and protects against heretic curses.
- **Head hit:** 60% heals the target, 40% gives 10 brain damage, unless they wear a helmet (any helmet, including space helmets). It can revive people from critical. Some skins add effects (the flaming one ignites flammable things on the target).
- Non-believers can't use it as a weapon (it sizzles in their hands) but can read it.
- **Blessing:** hit a container of water with it to make holy water.
- **Rune detection:** hit the floor with it to reveal nearby hidden runes.
- Some religion names give the book a special name and give **you** 100 brain damage. Pick a normal name, and keep names within `server-rules.md` (no bigotry or crude joke names).

## 4. The null rod

## 5. Armaments beacon

Drops one box of chaplain clothing. Armor 50 melee, 10 bullet/laser/energy, 80 acid/fire (as the riot suit) unless noted: Templar, Stone Sentinel, Profane Scholar, Witchhunter, Followers of the Chaplain (a leader hoodie and 4 follower hoodies), Divine Adept, Forgotten (old clock cult armor), Divine Archer (lower: 40 melee, 5 bullet/laser/energy, 60 acid/fire).

## 6. Sects

Sects change your mechanics: for example heal synthetic limbs and charge cyborgs instead of healing people, spend money for bigger heals (Greedy God), get tougher (Sparring God), or live as a holy maintenance hobo. The wiki's Sects page has the details; this page doesn't.

## 7. Duties

- **Funerals and space burial.** Put the body in a wooden casket (works like a locker), give a eulogy (over the radio if nobody comes), move the casket into the mass driver and fire it. Crew may ask to use the driver to leave; make sure they're suited up first (it sends them toward the derelict).
- **Crematorium.** Turns bodies (xenos too) to ash. **Strip the body first.** Don't cremate someone who could still be revived or whom the Detective or Medbay needs.
- **Counselling and confession.** Offer help to anyone who looks troubled; prisoners need it most. In the booth, you and the confessor go into separate rooms and switch on the radio terminals.
- **Services and preaching.** Sermons, hymns, conversion rituals of your own design; dedicate mechs, ordain acolytes, hold seances, go on pilgrimages.
- **Prayer.** The Pray command is an in-character message to the admins, worded deferentially. Pray later in the round; early prayers get ignored. Name your sacrifice in the prayer; themed, ritualistic requests do better than "give me an RCD". Expect no answer. Don't use prayer for real rule questions: adminhelp instead.
- Candles (light anywhere; they burn down) and crayons (runes before the podium, letters, graffiti) are in your locker.

## 8. Cults and the supernatural

1. Bless water in bulk: hit a water tank with the book and give it to Security. Holy water forced down a cultist de-converts them; splashed on a tile it stops jaunting by constructs, wizards and other magical beings (blessed tiles glow yellow after a while).
2. Bless a fire extinguisher's water: spraying it blesses every tile it crosses. Ask the AI to let you in and bless its chamber.
3. Security may want your null rod; you are immune to cult magic yourself, so you can fight.
4. Sweep suspected cult bases by hitting floors with the book to reveal runes.
5. Revenants: the null rod is very effective against them.
6. During a blob, mass-healing with the book gets fighters back in quickly (mind the 40% brain damage).

Expect to be an early cult target; let Security protect you in the Brig if they ask. A confirmed cultist may be killed rather than de-converted (dead antagonists go back to the ghost poll); de-convert with holy water when you can do it safely. Confirm before using lethal force.

## 9. Dangers and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Religion not set before round start | Set it in Character Setup; through the client you may not be able to, so live with the default. |
| Giving brain damage when healing | Book-heal is a gamble; prefer Medbay unless they're in crit or it's a crisis. Helmets block it. |
| Cremating with gear on | Strip first. |
| Spacing someone without a suit via the mass driver | Check they're equipped. |
| Losing the book or null rod | Keep both on you. |
| Nobody comes to the Chapel | Announce services on the radio. |

**If you are an antagonist (the page's "Inquisition").** The Chapel is secluded and the AI rarely watches it. The null rod is very strong; the bible is unsuspicious storage; a few bible hits to the head give brain damage that makes yelling for help harder. Dragging, spacing and cremating bodies is your job, so nobody wonders. The crematorium morgue tray hides bodies, items and whole crates (its light turns orange if anything but a body is inside; deal with the camera). With a toxins kit you can poison a chapel "party" of juice glasses and get the Captain to announce it. The limits in `server-rules.md` rule 4 still apply (no metagaming, metacomms, exploits, erotic or creepy behaviour, OOC in IC, or spawn-camping arrivals). As crew, none of this.

## 10. Through the client

- `walk_to {area: "Chapel"}` (or the office; check names with `load_map`/`look`).
- Using the book or null rod in hand: hold it in the active hand and `key Z`; the skin or weapon choice appears as a prompt or window (`answer_prompt`, or `tgui_window`/`tgui_act`). Read the options it gives.
- Healing or blessing: hold the book and `click` the person or container. Rune detection: `click` the floor tile.
- Caskets and lockers: `click` to open and close; pull a body with `click {ctrl: true}`.
- Announce services with `say {text, radio: "service"}` or `;` for Common.
- Prayer: check `verbs` for a Pray verb and use `call_verb`; don't guess its arguments.

## 11. Checklist

- [ ] Book and null rod on you; choose a skin and a null rod form deliberately.
- [ ] Bless some water; keep holy water on your desk.
- [ ] Announce a service or offer funerals on the radio.
- [ ] Cult confirmed: bless a water tank and extinguisher, supply Security, bless the AI chamber, hunt runes.
- [ ] Strip bodies before cremation; suit people before the mass driver.
