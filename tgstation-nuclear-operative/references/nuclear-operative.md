# Nuclear Operative (tgstation)

The wiki's guide to the Syndicate nuclear strike team. It is derived from the wiki page and is **not checked against the server's code**; prices, numbers and item lists drift between versions. **Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win.**

> **Rules.** You are a **team antagonist** (Rule 4 in `server-rules.md`): destroying the station is your objective, and you may kill, sabotage and blow things up to get it done. The limits that still apply: no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC or IC in OOC, no spawn-camping arrivals, and don't harm your team's work, directly or by doing nothing. Team goals outrank traitor goals in the priority list.

## 1. The role

A team of Syndicate operatives starts at its base off-station with a stolen nuclear fission explosive. The nuke can only be armed with the station's **nuclear authentication disk**, normally held by the Captain. Objectives, in order:

1. Retrieve the disk from the station.
2. Use it to arm the nuke (kept on the Infiltrator).
3. Escape alive on the Syndicate Infiltrator.

Workflow: **plan** → **order** gear → **infiltrate** (Infiltrator or assault pod) → **steal** the disk → **activate** the nuke on the station → **evacuate** to base. Take your time at the base; there is no rush before you leave.

## 2. Team, leader and radio

- Every operative has a red **S** icon by their head, visible to the team. Check for it before firing. The team shares a surname, set by the leader.
- **The leader** spawns behind the desk (inside the tables) and is the only one with access to the shutter button to the Infiltrator. Their headset has a loud mode (large text on the channel). The leader holds the war declaration tool and the **lead agent card**. If you are leader and don't want it, hand the card and the challenge item to someone else. **Listen to the leader**: the team discusses, the leader decides.
- **Radio:** your headset talks on the encrypted Syndicate channel with plain `;`. A Syndicate key in a normal headset uses `:t`. The channel is shared with any other Syndicate agents: Lavaland communications agents, traitors who bought a Syndicate encryption key, malfunctioning AIs. Cooperate with them if they speak up.
- Agree on **passwords** (for a door guard on the Infiltrator) and **safewords** (to recognise each other in disguise; short, or phonetic-alphabet) early, before anyone loses a headset. A **Codespeak Manual** (3 TC) makes your speech unintelligible to crew who steal a Syndicate key; use it in hand to learn, hit others (including cyborgs and animals) with it to teach them.

## 3. Plans

| Style | What it is |
|---|---|
| Surprise (classic) | Sneak in, sabotage comms and the AI, then burst out of maintenance after the disk holder. The most effective. |
| Stealth | Disguises and believable agent IDs; kill the disk holder and nuke without the crew knowing. Hard; needs coordination. |
| Split | Classic, plus one or more disguised operatives to grab the disk if the main push fails. |
| War | Leader declares war: very large TC bonus, station warned, assault delayed until the round is 20 minutes old. |
| Locker | Team in a locker, a disguised operative drags (or mails it via the Delivery Office) to the target. |
| Pod | Drop pod on the right side of the base slams into a chosen spot. One-way: plan an exit. |
| Fluke (murderbone) | Attack everyone. Usually fails: the crew's thrown toolboxes add up. |

**Declaring war:** the leader's tool alerts the station that you attack in about 20 minutes and gives the team around **280 TC** (scales with crew count), split evenly. Expect a fully armed station and a disk hidden in nested boxes, welded lockers or even inside the Clown's chest cavity.

## 4. Telecrystals and gear

Each operative starts with an uplink radio holding **25 TC**, plus a population-scaled bonus. Plan purchases as a team: cover **access, firepower, healing, distraction**. Everyone needs a **doormag or jaws of life**. Nuke ops cannot buy the chameleon kit; use a chameleon projector, stealth implant or changed clothes.

**Base:** the control room has the **Big Red Button** (Syndicate Detonator: detonates all active syndicate bombs at once) and Cayenne the carp. South is the **Equipment Room**: five armory closets, each with tactical turtleneck, black shoes, headset with Syndicate key, chest rig, operative holster (for large guns), pocket crowbar, night vision goggles, 9mm magazine, suspicious rope hook (climb up a z-level). Also an Ion Rifle (take it against silicons) and a YouTool vendor.

**Infiltrator tool storage:** NVGs, suspicious toolbox (wrench for a powersink), 2 syndicate bomb beacons (part of a plan, don't grab idly), 2 minibombs, 5 C4, syndicate medical duffel, military PDA with detomatix cartridge, tear gas and flashbang boxes, 5 pistol magazines, proximity sensors, signallers.

**Suit up for space:**
1. Elite MODsuit if bought, else a Syndicate MODsuit from the Infiltrator's EVA storage.
2. Put on the syndicate mask from your backpack box.
3. Suit in EVA mode, not combat.
4. Oxygen tank from the rack, fill it at the O2 canister, put it in suit storage, turn on internals.
5. Optional: jet harness instead of the suit jetpack (also works as internals).
6. Set the agent ID disguise (now or on station). Take NVGs if you have no thermals (essential with a powersink). Empty your bag of what you won't use.

### Explosives

- **C-4** (1 TC; bulk duffel 8 TC): use in hand to set the timer, then on a wall/target; 3 seconds uninterrupted to plant. Knocks down any wall and wounds whoever is behind it. Always carry some: a bolted airlock should never stop you.
- **Minibomb** (6 TC): 5-second fuse grenade, holes the floor to space.
- **Syndicate bomb** (11 TC): beacon-teleported; set timer (minimum 90), wrench it down, run. The Big Red Button sets them all off.
- **Grenadier's belt** (22 TC): 26 grenades (10 frag, plus minibombs, incendiaries, EMP, acid, gluon), screwdriver, multitool.

### Utility

- **Agent ID** (free): chameleon name/job, access to maintenance, the base and the Infiltrator. Use it on looted IDs to copy up to 5 access codes. **Never lose it**: it opens nuke storage and the pilot console.
- **Stimpack** (5 TC): 50u stimulants, speed, stun reduction, slight heal. The answer to stun batons.
- **Combat medkit** (4 TC): combat auto-injector, night vision med-HUD, patches, combat defib. At least one team medic.
- **Universal suppressor** (3 TC): silent firing; alt-click the gun to remove.
- **Chameleon projector** (7 TC): disguise as an item (default cigarette butt); walk speed, can't interact, AI can't track; breaks if picked up; gives a small click and sparkle on use.
- **Emag** (4 TC): no longer opens airlocks; breaks most other ID locks and subverts cyborgs.
- **Doormag** (3 TC): opens any airlock; 3 charges, each recharges in 3 minutes (examine to see). On bolted or welded doors it breaks the door shut for good.
- **Syndicate jaws of life** (4 TC): pry powered airlocks, unlimited, a few noisy seconds each.

### Mechs

- **Dark Gygax** (80 TC): incendiary carbine, flashbang launcher, teleporter, tesla relay, thrusters, leg actuators.
- **Dark Mauler** (140 TC): burst MG, scattershot, SRM-8 missiles, tesla relay, thrusters, smoke, ranged armor booster.
- Don't buy one unless you can make it pay. An ion rifle shuts mechs down; consider blowing out the back of the armory to take theirs first.

## 5. Syndicate cyborgs

Bought teleporters pull a ghost with the operative preference; if none, use the teleporter on the uplink to refund. They resist stuns, see operatives through disguises, have a 30000 W cell and ion thrusters, speak binary (so can eavesdrop on the AI, or blow your cover by accident). Weak to ion shots, EMPs, flashes and flashbangs. A roboticist can turn them crew-side with all modules: guard them. They can interact with machines remotely (open doors the AI bolted). Their **operative pinpointer** points to the nearest operative (dead or alive); keep it on.

| Model | Modules |
|---|---|
| Assault | e-sword, energy LMG, grenade launcher, emag, crowbar, extinguisher, flash, pinpointer |
| Medical | hypospray (nanites, pentetic acid, morphine), combat defib, medical beamgun, analyzer, surgical tools with energy saw, roller bed, crowbar, extinguisher, flash, pinpointer |
| Saboteur | improved tools, iron/glass/rod/cable synths, RTD, RCD (deconstructs reinforced walls), RPD, extinguisher, zipties, thermals, destination labeler (travel disposals), chameleon projector, flash, pinpointer. **No emag**: flash borgs and bring them to an operative who has one. |

To subvert a station borg: flash it, then emag, crowbar, emag.

## 6. Getting there

- **Infiltrator:** fly it from the cockpit shuttle console to a spot near the station; a second console sets a custom destination. Turrets fire penetrator rounds at non-operatives; one entrance with a blast door. Park out of sight of EVA, somewhere everyone will remember. Operatives get lost in deep space.
- **Assault pod:** whole team in, pick an area, land in seconds, crushing anything below; turrets inside. **One-way**: one operative must fly the Infiltrator near the station as the exit.

## 7. The disk

- Each operative has a syndicate PDA: turn it on, run **Fission360**, scan, select the nuke disk. Keep it visible; an arrow tracks the disk. An emagged PDA can download Fission360.
- The holder is usually Captain, HoP, HoS (or the Clown). Ambush them, lure them with a false alarm, blow the wall behind them with C4, or cause trouble in a secure area so they flee into your team.
- **Examine the disk** and check the pinpointer: tech disks and genetics disks are not the nuke disk.

## 8. Arming the nuke

Fetch the nuke from the Infiltrator's aft storage (insert the disk to release its bolts) and take it to the station.

1. Click it with an empty hand.
2. Insert the disk.
3. Enter the code and press enter. The code is in your notes (IC tab, notes verb).
4. Set the time.
5. Set it to **Armed**.
6. Optional: unanchor, move it (it keeps timing if you die), re-anchor.
7. **Take the disk out** and carry it off, or throw the crew's pinpointers off.
8. Back to the Infiltrator; wait for live teammates, then leave.

**Ignore the station's vault nuke**: its code doesn't exist unless an admin creates it. (Lone operatives use the vault's Self Destruct instead, see 10.) If your objective is to steal the plutonium core from the self-destruct, that is a traitor objective, not this guide.

## 10. Lone Operative

A single operative spawned from ghosts, rarely. **The longer the disk sits still, the likelier one is.** Starts in space already in a Syndicate MODsuit with an expanded oxygen tank: Makarov, knife, jet harness, agent ID, NVGs, pinpointer, uplink (traitor-sized budget), and a Bulldog shotgun. No ship, no nuke. Instead, steal the disk, insert it into the **Self Destruct Device in the station Vault**, enter the code and timer, and run: Delta alert puts every eye on the Vault. If the self-destruct goes off you win even if you die. Stealth works best; time matters because someone may notice the disk is gone.

## 11. Tactics and tips

- Set the agent ID to a believable crew name and a real job (e.g. Chemist).
- Starting gloves and MODsuit gloves are insulated.
- Thermals make you flashable even with the helmet on; NVGs don't.
- The black-and-red space suit draws less panic than the red MODsuit for a stealth op. Locker-room headsets have a Syndicate key; chameleon headsets don't.
- Subvert or kill the AI first (it reports you); syndiborgs or a sniper with thermals and penetrator rounds can do it quietly. Taking out telecomms confuses the crew. A powersink needs NVGs and a crowbar.
- Make sure the escape shuttle can't be called.
- C4 on the armory wall from space robs it quietly.
- Bring flashbangs if the station has cyborgs.
- No-slip shoes stop clown slips.
- Don't let yourself be stripped; your explosive implant works even in stun or crit. A macrobomb implant suits an operative who doesn't expect to live.
- Novelty buys: broken chameleon kit, old CentCom official gear (a fake "disk inspection" works only until the Captain calls CentCom), clown costume.

## 12. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Losing your agent ID | Keep it on you; without it you're locked out of the Infiltrator and nuke storage. |
| Friendly fire | Check for the red S before shooting. |
| Grabbing a fake disk | Examine it, confirm with Fission360. |
| Arming the nuke at your own base or off the station z-level | Arm it on the station. |
| Leaving the disk in the nuke | Take it out and carry it off. |
| Assault pod with no exit | One operative flies the Infiltrator close. |
| Lost Infiltrator / lost in space | Park somewhere memorable; use the pinpointer. |
| Stopped by a bolted door | Carry C4 or X4. Doormag on a bolted door breaks it shut. |
| Mech wasted by an ion rifle | Don't buy one without a plan; take the armory's ion gun first. |
| Syndiborg on binary gives you away | Tell borgs to stay quiet there. |

## 13. Fighting operatives (crew)

The station's defence is the disk. Keep it moving or hidden, take it on the shuttle (Crew Major). Ion weapons stop mechs and borgs; flashes and flashbangs stop borgs; riot shotguns go through energy shields. Stimpacks and mobs with batons are what operatives fear. See `tgstation-captain` and `tgstation-security-officer`.

## 14. Through the client

- Team talk: `say {text: "; plan is ..."}` (your headset routes `;` to the Syndicate channel), or `:t` with a Syndicate key in another headset.
- Uplink, PDA (Fission360), shuttle consoles and the nuke all open tgui windows: `tgui_window` to read them, `tgui_act` to press buttons. Read the window before acting; don't guess action names.
- The nuke code: `verbs` / `call_verb` for the notes verb (IC tab).
- Check `inventory` before every `click`: an item in hand is used on the target. Swap hands with `key {key: "X"}`, combat mode with `key {key: "F"}`.
- Moving in space: `move` steps; `walk_to` on the station once the map is loaded.

## 15. Checklist

- [ ] Leader known; plan agreed; passwords and safewords set.
- [ ] TC spent as a team: access (doormag/jaws) for everyone, a medic, anti-silicon option.
- [ ] Suit on, mask on, tank filled, internals on, EVA mode; NVGs; agent ID set.
- [ ] Fission360 tracking the disk.
- [ ] Exit route (Infiltrator parked or flown near the station).
- [ ] Disk examined; nuke armed on station; disk removed; everyone back aboard.
