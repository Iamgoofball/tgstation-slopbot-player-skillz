# Bitrunner (tgstation)

Derived from the wiki's Bitrunner page; not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win. The wiki's domain layouts are on a separate page ("Bitrunner domains") that this guide doesn't include.

## 1. The role in one paragraph

You are in Supply, under the Quartermaster. You load virtual domains on a quantum server, lie in a netpod to pilot an avatar through them, solve the puzzle or win the fight, carry the domain's encrypted cache back to the safehouse, and deliver the materials it produces on the station to Cargo. Runs are short, team play pays more, and damage to your avatar can hurt your real body. Difficulty: medium. Minimum: load a domain, get in a netpod, read the domain info, complete it, leave by the alert or hololadder, shut the domain down, and deliver the cache to Cargo.

## 2. Kit, access and radio

- **Access:** Cargo Bay, Cargo Office, Bitrunning Den, Maintenance, Mineral Storage, mining mech access; Mining Station and Mining Office as extras.
- **Radio:** Supply.
- Keep some **mannitol** (and the page mentions neurine) for brain damage; Chemistry can make mannitol.

## 3. The Bitrunning Den

| Machine | What it does |
|---|---|
| Quantum server | Loads one domain at a time; must cool down between runs. Upgrades: **capacitors** cut cooldown, **scanners** reveal more domain info, **servos** reduce injury from severed connections. Upgrading other machines does nothing. |
| Quantum console | Must be next to a server. Shows your **points** (top right; spent on domains; earned only by retrieving the cache), the **domain list** (free and entry-level ones are always available; detail depends on scanners), **connection info** (remaining connections, avatars and their damage, not always current), and the **loaded domain** (halt it here; connected avatars are warned first). A **random domain** gives bonus points (not for entry-level ones). |
| Netpod | Drag yourself into it while a domain is loaded. Resists damage; warns you if damaged or crowbarred open. Slowly heals you; ejects you when out of power. Right-click to choose your avatar's job (starting gear); some domains force one. |
| Byteforge | Spawns the cache's materials on the station. The cache is symbolic: you can't carry items or people out in it. |

## 4. A run

1. At the console, pick a domain (expand its info for task and rewards). Load it.
2. Optionally right-click a netpod to set your avatar's job. Put tech disks in your bag (§6).
3. Drag yourself into the netpod.
4. In the safehouse: gear for the mission, marked extraction tiles (circuit tiles; examine to confirm), and hololadders (exits). Avatars are limited by the hololadder landmarks.
5. Use the **domain info** ability: the clues are usually in the first paragraph.
6. Find the cache (a sealed crate), drag it onto an extraction tile in the safehouse.
7. Leave: click the completion alert, or walk over/click a hololadder. Avatar death also returns you, unsafely.
8. Shut the domain down at the console and take the cache's output to Cargo.

Rewards scale with speed, difficulty, threats handled and the number of players.

## 5. Dangers

- Avatar damage reflects onto your body; healing does not. Toxin and suffocation don't carry over. Taking damage too fast, or being knocked out, disconnects you.
- **Forced disconnects cause brain damage:** avatar death, pod destroyed or pried, server or pod losing power. Servo upgrades reduce it.
- After a severed connection, **stay in the netpod until it ejects you**; it heals everything except organ damage. Then treat brain damage with mannitol, or a coworker at a surgery table.
- **Space Police:** some domains invite ghosts to take over enemies and kill you. Ghosts can also help. The page says "all Bitrunners are valid inside domains": that applies only inside the simulation; on the station the normal `server-rules.md` apply to you and to everyone else.
- Don't trust any mob in a domain, dead or alive, to stay non-sentient.
- Stuck as something useless in a domain (say, a gondola)? Dying leaves the simulation, unsafely.

## 6. NexaPoints, the NexaCache vendor and disks

Completing domains earns NexaPoints for the NexaCache vendor. Orders go on the cargo shuttle, or come at once by express pod for extra. Categories: Misc (cosmetic, snacks), Tech (disks), Bepis Tech (old toy prizes).

**Disks.** Use a disk in hand to choose its item or ability (one-time choice), then carry it in your bag or pockets when entering. All disks you carry load; items appear in hand or at your feet. You can't stack copies of the same disk and choice. Gear disks give items; program disks give action-button abilities with short cooldowns.

| Disk | Cost | Options and notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gear: Basic | 487 | Medbeam gun (good vs many weak enemies), infinite pizza box (slow self-heal), one C4 (domain walls are mostly indestructible) |
| Gear: Complex | 812 | Chainsaw, automatic pistol, hardlight sword (ninja-katana damage, no block) |
| Gear: Advanced | 1300 | Tesla cannon (weak), double-bladed e-sword (reflects lasers), the Stinger (fast, bee venom) |
| PKA Mods: Simple | 487 | Range, damage, cooldown, AOE, human passthrough. Mostly redundant: PKA domains give range/damage/cooldown mods already. |
| PKA Mods: Premium | 1040 | Repeater cooldown, lifesteal crystal, resonator blasts (good); pressure mod and bounty hunter kit (little use) |
| Crusher Mods: Simple | 487 | Watcher wing, magmawing, legion skull (DPS), wolf ear (movement) |
| Crusher Mods: Premium | 1040 | Icewing, colossus tubes, blooddrunk eye, ash drake spike, bubblegum claws (healing), hierophant talisman, ice demon cube (guaranteed freeze) |
| Program: Basic | 487 | Conjure cheese, basic heal (good vs many weak enemies) |
| Program: Complex | 974 | Fireball (best vs Space Police: damage, knockdown, flash), lightning bolt, forcewall (break line of sight to heal) |
| Program: Elite | 1625 | Shapeshift dragon or polar bear (slow; mostly for Space Police) |

Gimmick disks are mostly for fun: Alchemist (812: plague doctor suit, two basic chemicals, 20-50 u of a random unlabelled one), Rogue (812: knife and outfit), Healer (812: surgery medkit, bee larva honey, rod of asclepius, which makes you a pacifist), Wizard (812: fake robes, Untie Shoes and Smoke spells), Boxer (487), Skater (487: Wheeley-Heeleys, the fastest permanent speed if you don't crash), Archer (487: longbow, blazing arrows; decent), Fisher (487: premium bait, an IED), Gamer (487: laptop with arcade and mafia).

Every domain is meant to be completable with the gear inside it; ditch disk gear that doesn't fit.

## 7. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stopping a domain before the cache is out | No points; finish or accept the loss. |
| Climbing out of the pod after a sever | Wait for it to eject and heal you. |
| No cure for brain damage | Stock mannitol; ask Chemistry. |
| More bitrunners than pods | Build a surgery table in the den; the one outside treats brain damage and heals early leavers. |
| Leaving the domain loaded | Shut it down at the console. |
| Cache not delivered | Take the byteforge output to Cargo. |
| Wasting points on random disks | Pick by domain: heals for swarms, fireball for Space Police. |

## 8. Working with the crew

Deliver materials to Cargo and keep the Quartermaster informed on the Supply channel. Bring friends: more runners, more reward. Ask Chemistry for mannitol and Robotics or Science for server upgrade parts.

## 9. Through the client

- `walk_to {area: "Bitrunning Den"}`; `look` to find the server, console, netpods and byteforge.
- Console: `click` it, read the domains and actions with `tgui_window`, load or stop with `tgui_act` using the actions it lists.
- Enter a netpod: `drag` yourself onto it (as with the strip menu in `tgstation-security-officer` §9). Set the avatar job with `click {right: true}` on the pod.
- In the domain, the domain info and completion alert come as an action button and an alert: `screen` shows the buttons, `answer_alert` or `click` the alert to leave.
- Drag the cache with `click {ctrl: true}` (pull) to the extraction tiles; `key H` stops pulling.
- `status` before and after a run to check for brain damage.
- Report on Supply with `say {text, radio: "supply"}`.

## 10. Checklist

- [ ] Mannitol on hand; disks chosen and in your bag.
- [ ] Domain loaded; avatar job set; in the pod.
- [ ] Read the domain info; find the cache; extract it.
- [ ] Leave safely; shut the domain down.
- [ ] Cache output to Cargo; check your health.
