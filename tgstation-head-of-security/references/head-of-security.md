# Head of Security (tgstation)

Derived from the wiki's Head of Security page; not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win. Arrests, searches, the brig timer, records and Space Law are covered in `tgstation-security-officer`; this guide adds what is different about leading Security.

**Confirmed antagonists.** Once you have determined beyond doubt that someone is an antagonist, you and your officers may kill them. Most antagonists can't be safely contained, and a dead antagonist goes back to the ghost poll for midround antagonists and events, so killing a confirmed antagonist is normal and expected. Confirm first; until then, non-lethal arrest and Space Law apply. Tell your officers this at shift start.

## 1. The role in one paragraph

You head the Security department, answer to the Captain, and rank above the Warden, the officers and the Detective. Your job is to keep your people alive, working together and communicating, to keep them from abusing the crew ("shitcurity"), to make sure every department is protected, and to point Security at the big threats rather than petty crime. You watch over the Brig, the Bridge and the Courtroom. You are not meant to hunt suspects yourself except when it is urgent or in places your officers can't enter. Difficulty: very hard. The wiki's minimum: keep your team alive, together, talking, and chasing the bigger fish.

## 2. Kit, access and radio

- **Access:** all of Security (Armory, Brig, Security Office), Coroner Office, your office, Maintenance, Medbay, Morgue, Research, Bridge, Engineering, Construction, Cargo Office, Mining, Gateway, personal lockers, weapon permit. Don't lean on your access when an officer can go instead.
- **Radio:** Security (`:s`) and Command (`:c`).
- **Your locker:** X-01 Multiphase Energy Gun (three settings), telescopic riot shield, mindshield implants, a box of flashbangs, the nuclear disk pinpointer, a full security belt, gorilla gloves (in the garment bag), alternate outfits, and misc gear (loudspeaker, medal box; varies by map). Take most of it.
- **Your office** (inside the Security Office): weapons, devices, a recharger, both kinds of security terminal, the HoS MODsuit (better armor and modules), your stamp, a key terminal, a window shutter button, an ID console with a trim imprinter, and a box of armbands. Your desk announcement system works even with telecomms down.

## 3. Your people

| Who | What to do with them |
|---|---|
| Security Officers | Each gets a department assignment on joining. Correct them firmly when they misbehave; brig them briefly if they persist; have a persistently abusive or incompetent officer demoted to assistant. Go easy on new players. |
| Warden | Your right hand and next in line if you die. Make sure they don't set or back unfair brig times and don't leave the Brig while it holds prisoners. Otherwise let them run it. |
| Detective | Works with a lot of autonomy. Let them be; call them on the radio when you need a scan; send officers when they uncover something big (changelings). |
| Lawyers | Watch Security for violations. Your authority over them is questionable; you may remove their Security radio access if it becomes a problem. Don't refuse clients their right to counsel. A lawyer caught committing crimes is treated like any criminal. |

**Department assignments.** Most officers guard one of Medbay, Research, Engineering or Supply. They get a small office there, limited access, that department's radio channel and a color-coded armband. Make sure each department has someone; move more officers to a hotspot, but only the HoP can give proper access. Department guards should build rapport with their department so it cooperates when a big threat arrives.

## 4. The Brig and prisoners

- Security, heads of staff and the Lawyer can enter the Brig hallway; only Security can use the cell controls. Cell windows are electrified.
- **Set a time on the cell timer, or the door doesn't lock.** Put a portable flasher on the door or cuff the prisoner to the bed while you search them. Remove weapons, toolbelts and insulated gloves, or an engineer or cargo worker can escape.
- **Prison Wing** (upper left of the Warden's Office): as many cells as the brig, real airlocks, lockdown shutters, no automatic timer. Don't perma someone without a very good reason. Muzzles and straitjackets are kept there.
- **Labor camp (gulag)** on Lavaland: escape is nearly impossible.
- **Execution:** the prisoner transfer chamber is left of the Prison Wing. Keep executions away from Asimov silicons (don't discuss them on the Security radio). A confirmed antagonist may be executed rather than held; for ordinary crew criminals, follow Space Law.
- **Paddy** (Brig mech bay): an APLU exosuit with a hydraulic claw and a disabler shotgun. The claw loads up to 4 people and cuffs them (cuffing can be switched off to ferry officers to a raid). Assign it to an officer or ride it; have Robotics upgrade it; **set an ID or DNA lock** or it gets stolen.

## 5. Understaffing

- **No Warden:** you watch the Brig and Prison Wing; check on them often.
- **No Detective:** take the spare forensic scanner from the Detective's office and give it to an officer (or scan yourself, and teach someone).
- **Few officers:** deputize crew. Give them Security access at your ID console, an armband as a badge, gear and cuffs, a briefing, and consider a mindshield and/or tracking implant. Deputies may be inexperienced or traitors; you answer for them.

## 6. Lockdown

Controls in the Warden's Office and your office drop blast doors that seal off parts of the Brig. Add security barricades at entrances and use the Security Door Control (usually the Warden's) to bolt chosen doors.

## 7. Threats

Tell your officers the round's threat as soon as you confirm it.

| Threat | What to do |
|---|---|
| Revolution | You are a target. Don't charge out at every report of flashing; a traitor may fake a revolution to lure you. Keep moving. De-convert with a mindshield implant (reliable, limited supply) rather than beating them (risks killing). Anyone who rejects the implant is a head revolutionary: confirmed, so kill or exile them; the revolution only ends when the heads are out. **Don't turtle; you lose the war of attrition.** |
| Cult | Secure the Chaplain in the Brig at once: holy water forced down a cultist de-converts them and makes floor tiles unusable for wraith jaunts, and the null rod blocks magic. Nothing but the Chaplain's tools protects you from a cultist's stun. The AI can't see runes but can report and lock down gatherings (a P.A.L.A.D.I.N. lawset helps); wraiths kill AIs easily. Fight constructs with lasers from range; juggernauts deflect energy, so use ballistics (buckshot riot shotgun, auto rifle). Constructs in numbers: call evacuation. No Chaplain: Cargo sells religious supply crates; Botany can mutate holy melons (holy water; full anti-magic when held). |
| Nuclear operatives | Secure the disk (get the pinpointer from the Captain if they forget). Hiding it fails; the ops have pinpointers. Taking it off the z-level or destroying it respawns it on station, the worst case. Bring the Captain to the Brig, summon officers with the desk announcer, hand out bulletproof armor, lethals and perhaps the ion rifle, arm reliable crew as meat shields. Expect the armory to be raided. A lost disk can be chased back; if it leaves on the emergency shuttle you are fine. Fallback: evacuate to the mining station or gulag. |
| Blob | Empty the armory and hand out energy weapons; lock away disablers and shotguns (useless on biomass). Riot gear is strong against some chemical variants. **Lock the ion rifle in a locker.** Unlock laser crates from Cargo (few jobs can), fight, and carry rechargers to the front. |

## 8. Tools and tips

- **Implants** (Armory; more from Cargo):
  - *Chemical:* holds 50 u injected while still cased; triggered by the implantee or a prisoner control console.
  - *Tracking:* distance and direction to the target, messages to the implantee from the Brig console, active 10 minutes after death. Use for private orders while telecomms is down, and for changeling checks (an implant you didn't give, or a missing one you did).
  - *Exile:* can't fly mining shuttles or return through the Gateway.
  - *Bluespace grounding:* blocks most teleports and jaunts and stuns 5 seconds.
  - *Beacon:* a teleporter destination (tracking implants no longer are).
- Label armory items and anything you hand out.
- Gorilla gloves help against energy swords: in throw mode, right click to tackle.
- A sawn-off riot shotgun (unload it first, then use a circular saw) fits in a backpack.
- Disablers from Cargo are cheap and safe to arm civilians with: 20 shots, three hits before more than a slow, easy to identify.
- Borrow a portable crew monitor from the CMO to watch your officers' life signs. Disguised changelings won't show, and a changeling can get a mindshield.
- Your office pet (Sergeant Araneus, or Lisa the carp on some maps) can patrol with a sentience potion from Xenobiology.
- A sleep-gas tank (20% O2, 80% N2O at 101 kPa, with mask and internals) keeps a dangerous prisoner asleep and slows a changeling's revival (adrenals still let one wake and switch off internals), buying time to reach the gibber or cremator.
- A chemical implant loaded with Chemistry's deadliest 50 u works as a lethal parole implant.
- With the gibber and cremator gone, push a confirmed changeling under an arriving shuttle (the gulag shuttle is most reliable) to gib it.

## 9. Dangers and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Cell timer not set; prisoner walks out | Always set a time. |
| Prisoner escapes with tools or insulated gloves | Strip tools and gloves before cell time. |
| Officers fighting each other or abusing crew | Build team spirit, correct early, demote repeat offenders. |
| Hunting suspects yourself and dying | Direct officers; act yourself only when urgent. |
| Running toward every rev report | Verify first; keep moving. |
| Ion rifle in the wrong hands during a blob | Lock it away. |
| Paddy stolen | ID or DNA lock it. |
| Executing or perma-brigging crew for minor crimes | Space Law for crew; lethal force is for confirmed antagonists. |
| Brigging a confirmed antagonist who then escapes | Most can't be held; kill confirmed antagonists. |
| Killing on suspicion | Confirm beyond doubt first. |

## 10. Through the client

- `walk_to {area: "..."}` to reach your office, the Brig or the Bridge; `look` and `search` to find people and lockers.
- Command your people with `say {text, radio: "security"}`; Command channel with `:c`.
- `inventory` shows what you took from your locker; `key Z` uses the held item (examine the X-01 to check its setting).
- The ID console, records and brig timer are tgui windows: `tgui_window` lists their actions and `tgui_act` drives them. Read action names from the window; don't guess.
- Arrests, the strip menu and fighting through the client are in `tgstation-security-officer` §9.
