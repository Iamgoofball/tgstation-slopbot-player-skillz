# Changeling (tgstation)

Taken from the /tg/station wiki's Changeling page, not checked against the server's code. `crew-basics.md` and `server-rules.md` win where they disagree. A changeling is a **lone antagonist**: pursue your objectives by any means, killing included. Rule 4 frees a lone antagonist from most rules; the limits that still apply (`server-rules.md`, section 4) are: no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC or IC in OOC, and no spawn-camping arrivals. Crew may not hunt a suspected changeling without reasonable cause, but once you're exposed, expect the crew to try to kill you and destroy the body.

## 1. The role

An alien predator that copies the shape, voice and look of its victims. You spawn in your normal job with your victim's identity. You steal DNA, impersonate people, and use chemicals for stings and body changes. Other changelings are linked to you through the hivemind but are not allies: absorbing one boosts your powers a lot, and they know it too.

## 2. Resources

- **Chemicals:** regenerate about 0.5 per second. Every ability below lists its cost.
- **Evolution points:** start at 10. More only by absorbing another changeling (+5 each). Spend them in the **Cellular Emporium** (action button, upper left).
- **Readapt:** absorbing someone gives one refund of all evolved abilities (back to 10 points, or more if you've absorbed lings). Only one unused readapt stored at a time.
- **DNA:** up to 7 stored disguises; newer ones overwrite the oldest, but all still count for objectives.
- **Hivemind:** default `:g`. Changelings are immune to each other's stings and notice them: a target that doesn't react might be a ling.
- **Fire:** most abilities don't work while you are on fire (the table says which do).
- **BZ gas** drains your chemicals fast. Wear internals on Lavaland.

## 3. Objectives

| Objective | How |
|---|---|
| Absorb 5–7 DNA strands | Extract DNA sting is easiest. |
| Extract more genomes than any other ling | Sting a lot, or kill the rival. |
| Assassinate / maroon a person | Normal means or abilities. |
| Absorb another changeling | Kill and absorb one. |
| Steal an object | You're told what. |
| Escape alive | Be on the shuttle or a pod when it reaches Central Command. |
| Escape with identity | As above, transformed into the target, wearing an ID with their name. |

## 4. Getting DNA

- **Extract DNA Sting** (25 chems): instant, no alert, unlimited samples.
- **Absorb:** strangle the victim (aggressive grab to neck), click Absorb, sit through three long progress bars. Gives DNA, some memories (uplink codes, objectives), a little chemicals and a readapt. The victim becomes a husk that can't be revived by normal means (only cloning pods/replica pods or magic). Absorbing a burn/space husk gives no genome. Absorbing a changeling gives all its chemicals and DNA, raises max storage, and +5 evolution points.

Stings: **Alt-click or middle-click** with the sting prepared, up to 2 tiles. Only one sting prepared at a time; chemicals spent only on use.

## 7. Tactics

- **Take few abilities at round start.** They're locked until you absorb someone, and absorbing is slow, obvious and easy to interrupt. Keep points free to adapt.
- A **wallet** (crew quarters cabin closets, or leather) in the ID slot holds 4 IDs; all work for access and don't change your shown name.
- Fleshmend is for fights; heal outside fights with Reviving Stasis to save points.
- While absorbing, Chameleon Skin hides you from anyone who walks in.
- Transformation Sting: turn the person you're impersonating into someone else so you're the only "them"; transform a caught ling ally in a chaotic scene. Wear generic clothes (arrivals, locker room), break line of sight, transform, double back.
- Space: drift as a reviving corpse, or Fleshmend plus fire suit; Void Adaptation; clown shuttle corpses; spaced crew are identities nobody will miss. Cryo sting is strong on people in space suits.
- A space burn husk stays husked after revival; transform away and back to clear it.
- Escapes: Blind or Cryo Sting then Lesser Form.
- Mimic Voice with Digital Camouflage (or a subverted AI) turns the crew against each other.
- The bar drink **Changeling Sting** speeds chemical regen; ordering it is suspicious.
- Flesh clothes can't be removed by anything (even facehuggers fail if your disguise's headgear blocks them), which is also how you get caught on a strip search.
- Hivemind text: don't announce crimes there carelessly.

## 8. Fighting a changeling (crew)

- **Kill it for good:** gib it (gibber; a tier 2+ manipulator gibs clothed people) or cremate it in the chapel. Shuttle-gibbing works (mining shuttle is quick). Debraining does nothing: lings don't need heads.
- **Fire** disables most abilities. **BZ** drains chemicals; a BZ/O2/N2O tank on a cuffed ling holds it unless it has Void Adaptation or Biodegrade.
- **Never fight one alone.** One stuns, the other cuffs; then gib or cremate.
- **Tells:** clothing you can't remove on a strip search; no reaction to a sting; inhuman movement on examine (Digital Camo); arm blades.
- **Cryostung?** Take off armour, helmet, hardsuit and other insulating gear at once, or you freeze.
- Stunned near an intercom: `:i` still talks through it.
- Absorbed victims can come back as podpeople from blood drawn before the absorb (exotic seeds crate, replica pod).
- Potassium/water grenades no longer gib lings.
- **Rules:** you need a reasonable cause to go after a suspected ling. Once you are certain someone is an antagonist, lethal force is fine and expected: most can't be held safely, and a dead antagonist goes back into the ghost pool for midround roles. With a ling, finish the job: gib or cremate.

## 9. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Spending all 10 points at round start | Keep points for later; you can't change without a readapt. |
| Using abilities while on fire | Put yourself out first; only some work burning. |
| Reviving at the wrong moment | Wait in stasis until no one is guarding the body. |
| Headslug with no corpse in reach | Use Last Resort only when a corpse is close. |
| Arm blade in public | It outs you; retract it. |
| Getting gibbed or cremated | Avoid Security custody; escape before transport to the chapel. |
| Lavaland without internals | BZ drains your chemicals. |
| (Crew) dueling a ling alone | Bring a buddy; stun, cuff, gib. |

## 10. Working with others

- Other changelings: you may team up or betray them. Absorbing one is a big reward, for you or for them.
- Traitors: no obligation either way. Rules priority: antagonist conversions sit above traitors; follow team goals first if you hold several roles.

## 11. Through the client

- Ability buttons are HUD actions: list them with `screen` and press with `click`. The Cellular Emporium and the Transform menu open windows: `tgui_window` to read, `tgui_act` to evolve or pick a DNA.
- Sting: prepare it (HUD button), then `click` the target within 2 tiles with `alt: true`.
- Absorb: aggressive grab to the neck (see `tgstation-client` for grabbing), then the Absorb button; `wait` through the progress bars and check `status`.
- Hivemind: `say` with text starting `:g`.
- Chemicals and points: `statpanel`.
- Crew: `say` with `radio: "security"` to report; `examine` for inhuman movement.

## 12. Checklist

- [ ] Objectives read; identity and job known.
- [ ] Extract DNA Sting evolved (free); 2–4 points spent, the rest kept.
- [ ] A wallet with spare IDs.
- [ ] DNA from 5–7 people if that's an objective.
- [ ] Escape plan: stasis, Lesser Form, Biodegrade or Last Resort.
- [ ] For escape-with-identity: transformed into the target, wearing their ID, on the shuttle.
