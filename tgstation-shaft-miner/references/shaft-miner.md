# Shaft Miner

## The job in one paragraph

You get the station its minerals. Everything the station prints (tools, defibrillators, stock parts, machine boards,
mech parts) is made from materials in one shared store, the **ore silo** in the Vault. Every lathe draws from it,
and nearly everything except iron and glass comes from you. You work on **Lavaland**, a volcanic planet under the
station, reached by the **mining shuttle** from the Mining Office in Cargo. There you do two things. You **tap ore
vents**: defend a drone while waves of fauna attack it. A tapped vent then sends boulders to the station by itself
for the rest of the round, and the station's machines turn them into silo materials with nobody hauling anything.
You also **mine ore** from the rock and bring it back to the **ore redemption machine** in Cargo, which also feeds
the silo. Success: the silo fills with silver, gold, plasma, titanium, uranium and some diamond. The Scientist,
Medbay and the Engineers can print what they need, and you come home alive. Lavaland kills careless miners: lava,
ash storms, fauna and unbreathable air. Your head is the **Quartermaster**. If no Quartermaster is on shift, report
to the Head of Personnel, or to the Captain if there's no Head of Personnel either. Other miners and bitrunners may
be on shift too.

## Where things are

### On MetaStation

MetaStation has no "Mining Dock" area. The dock is the west end of the **Mining Office**.

- **Mining Office**: through the two "Mining Office" doors in the **Cargo Bay**'s north wall. From the Central
  Primary Hallway: the **Cargo Lobby**, then its "Cargo Bay" door. Inside:
  - North wall, west part: three **miner's equipment lockers** (Mining Dock access) and a mining wardrobe.
  - South wall, west part: the **mining shuttle console** and the Mining Requests Console.
  - West end: the **"Mining Dock"** glass door, a small cycling airlock, then the external **"Mining Dock Airlock"**.
    The mining shuttle docks beyond it. **When the shuttle isn't docked, that outer door opens onto space.** A rack
    by the door holds a pickaxe and a shovel.
  - East part: the **boulder belt**. The **boulder retrieval matrix** (BRM) sits on a conveyor. The belt runs west and
    then south through the **boulder refinery** and the **boulder smelter**. The **conveyor switch** (a lever) is
    just west of the belt.
  - North-east: the **mining equipment order console** (your shop), beside the bitrunners' console and the
    **Bitrunning Den**.
- **Ore redemption machine** (ORM): built into the wall between the Cargo Bay and the Cargo Lobby, at the south end
  of the Cargo Bay's east wall. Its **intake is the Cargo Bay floor tile just west of it**: ore dropped there or an
  ore box pulled onto it goes straight in. A windoor covers each face: "Cargo Security Window" on the Cargo Bay side
  (cargo access) and "Ore Redemption Window" on the lobby side (public). Sheets you release come out on the lobby side.
- **Ore silo**: in the **Vault** (Command). You can't get in and don't need to.
- **Quartermaster's Office**: off the Cargo Bay and the Cargo Lobby.
- **Common Mining Dock**: an external airlock in the **Port Primary Hallway**. The public **lavaland shuttle** docks
  there. Its console is on board, and it flies to the "Mining base public dock" at the Lavaland base's lounge. It
  needs no access, which makes it a backup route and the way a rescuer reaches you.
- The **mining shuttle** (area "Mining Shuttle") has its own **mining shuttle console**, an **ore box**, emergency
  oxygen tanks, breath masks and 2 emergency pickaxes.

### On Lavaland: the mining station

The mining shuttle lands at the "lavaland mine" dock, at the south end of the **production building**. The living
building is to the west and south-west. **The two buildings connect only across open ground**, through "Mining
External Airlock" doors, and that ground is exposed to ash storms.

Production building:
- **Mining Station Production Wing** (the main hall, entered through the "Mining Shuttle Airlock" doors):
  - West wall: the **mining shuttle console**, a **barometric monitor** (green, yellow, red: the storm light) and a
    **DeForest first aid station** (empty-hand click to heal; right-click for gauze).
  - Mid-hall tables: a **mining equipment order console** and a **first-aid kit**. Emergency closets and **ore boxes**
    stand around the hall. The old furnace in the "Processing Area" isn't linked to the silo; ignore it.
  - Exits: north through two pairs of "Mining External Airlock" (a shower between them) to the **active ore vent**
    just outside; west through two more pairs.
- **Mining Station EVA** (door "Mining Station EVA", east side of the hall): three **mining suit storage units** (each
  holds an **explorer suit** and an **explorer gas mask**), an **oxygen tank dispenser** (full-size tanks), a second
  mining equipment order console, and a table with **4 GPS units**, 2 pickaxes, a toolbox and a first-aid kit.
- Also off the hall: Mining Station Mech Bay (west) and Mining Station Production Storage (north-west).

Living building:
- **Mining Station Public Lounge** (large, south). The public "Lavaland Shuttle Airlock" and the lavaland shuttle
  console are at its east end.
- **Mining Station Emergency Medbay** ("Infirmary" door off the lounge): 2 emergency medkits, bandages, a bottle of
  epinephrine pills, a DeForest first aid station and 2 emergency beds.
- **Mining Station Public Storage** ("Equipment Storage", off the lounge): 4 racks, each with a mining satchel,
  pickaxe, manual mining scanner, flashlight and optical meson scanner; 4 GPS units; 2 emergency oxygen tanks.
- Mining Station Cafeteria (microwave, donk pockets), Mining Station Living Quarters, Mining Station Hydroponics.
- **Mining Station Communications** (north maintenance; telecomms access): the **relay that carries your headset to the
  station**. If it loses power, your radio goes silent.

Elsewhere: the **Labor Camp** (prisoners mining under guard) is to the west; stay out of it. The **Necropolis**, home of
the Legion, is at the far north edge of the map; never go there. Everything between is generated at round start:
caves, lava rivers, ruins (their areas are all named "Unexplored Location"), fauna, ore vents.

## How your work reaches the station

At round start every station lathe linked itself to the ore silo: the protolathe, every techfab, and the ORM, boulder
refinery and boulder smelter in Cargo. So:
- **Ore put into the ORM becomes silo materials at once** (1 ore = 1 sheet's worth), printable by the Scientist a
  second later. **Nobody needs sheets delivered by hand** unless they lack a lathe.
- **Boulders from tapped ore vents** come by themselves: each tapped vent makes a boulder every 60 s (up to 10 waiting
  on its tile), the BRM teleports them onto the belt, the **refinery** takes out glass, plasma, diamond and bluespace,
  the **smelter** iron, titanium, silver, gold and uranium, and both feed the silo. (The wiki says to link them to the
  silo with a multitool first; in this version the mapped ones are linked already.)
- One vent by the base is **already tapped** (iron and glass), but the BRM and conveyor start **off**.
- **To check the silo**, open the ORM: its materials list is the silo. "No ore silo connection is available" means
  something is wrong: tell the Quartermaster.

## Core procedures

### 1. Shift start (on the station)

1. Set your suit sensors to tracking (crew-basics). The station's crew monitor still can't see you on Lavaland (see
   "Getting found").
2. `walk_to {area: "Mining Office"}` if you aren't there. From one **miner's equipment locker** take the
   **proto-kinetic accelerator** (KA), **optical meson scanner** (wear it), **bluespace shelter capsule**, **manual
   mining scanner** and **compact pickaxe**. Leave the Landing Field Designator, the sandbags and the other lockers.
3. **Turn on the boulder belt**: **right-click the boulder retrieval matrix** (automatic retrieval on; examining it
   shows "there are N boulders available to teleport"), then click the **conveyor switch** once. Iron and glass now
   flow into the silo by themselves.
4. **Redeem your voucher**: click the mining equipment order console with the **mining voucher** in hand and pick a
   kit in the radial menu. **"Survival Capsule and Explorer's Webbing"** (a second shelter, a gear belt) is the
   safest. **"Crusher Kit"** gives a kinetic crusher, a pocket extinguisher and a bigger pocket tank.
5. Supply: "Shaft Miner heading to Lavaland, boulder belt is on." Science (`:n`): ask what they're short of.
6. Board the shuttle (procedure 2).

### 2. The mining shuttle (both ways)

- The shuttle starts the round **docked at the station**. **Board it first, then use the console on the shuttle.** The
  Mining Office console would send it away without you; it's for calling the shuttle back from Lavaland.
- The Mining Dock doors are a **cycling airlock**: a bumped door says "Pressurizing airlock." or "Docking sequence
  initiated". Wait up to 10 s and walk into it again. Never open the outer door while the shuttle is away (space).
- Console (tgui "ShuttleConsole"): `status` is Idle, In Transit or Recharging, and `locations` lists destinations.
  `tgui_act move {"shuttle_id": "mining_away"}` flies to Lavaland ("lavaland mine"), `{"shuttle_id": "mining_home"}`
  back to the station ("SS13: Mining Dock"). The trip takes about 15-20 seconds.
- At Lavaland, walk north through the "Mining Shuttle Airlock" doors into the Production Wing, where the air is fine.
  The shuttle stays where you left it; the console in either place calls it.

### 3. Gearing up at the Lavaland base

1. `load_map` the Lavaland file (see "Through the client").
2. **Mining Station EVA**: click a mining suit storage unit; in its radial menu pick "open", then click it again and
   pick "suit", then "mask" (never "disinfect"). Wear the **explorer suit** (melee 30, fire 50, bomb 50 armour,
   fireproof; raise the hood with its "Toggle Hood" action) and the **explorer gas mask**.
3. Put a full **oxygen tank** from the tank dispenser in the **suit storage** slot (the emergency tank stays in your
   backpack as a spare). Internals on **before** you step outside, off indoors (crew-basics, "Hunger, cold, heat, air").
4. Take a **GPS**, use it in hand and `rename` it to your name, so others can find your signal.
5. Use the **automatic mining scanner** in hand once to switch it on; it pulses by itself, even from your backpack.
6. Outside, keep the KA in your active hand and the capsule and medipen within reach.

### 4. Mining rock for ore

- **Rock:** basalt (4 s to dig with a pickaxe), **siderite** (red rock, 25% slower) and **shale** (75% slower). About
  7% of rock tiles hold ore, clustered in veins. **Ore rock looks exactly like plain rock.** Only the scanner's pulse
  shows it: each ore tile glows for 3.5 s, within 4 tiles for the automatic scanner (every 5 s) or 7 for the advanced
  one (bought; every 3.5 s). The manual scanner pulses when you use it in hand. Ore is also denser around ore vents.
- **The KA is your best digging tool.** Each shot mines the first rock tile in its path, up to 3 tiles away, and the
  gun recharges in 1.6 s. A pickaxe takes 4 s per basalt tile.
- Each ore tile drops 1-5 ore, which your satchel picks up as you walk over it. When it's full, empty it into an
  **ore box** by clicking the box with it. Better, **pull an ore box** (ctrl-click) while you mine: pickups go
  straight into it. Boxes are on the shuttle and in the Production Wing.
- Digging the floor gives volcanic ash (glass), but the starter vent already supplies iron and glass: go for the
  valuable ores.

| Ore (on the floor) | Points at the ORM, each | Where it's common | What the station uses it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| iron ore | 1 | everywhere | everything printed; plasteel (with plasma) |
| sand pile / volcanic ash | 1 | dig any floor | glass |
| plasma ore | 15 | shale, basalt | plasteel and plasma glass, Engineering's generator fuel, research |
| silver ore | 16 | siderite, basalt | **defibrillators** (with gold), medical scanners, stock parts |
| gold ore | 18 | shale, basalt | defibrillators, circuit parts, capacitors |
| titanium ore | 50 | siderite, basalt | Jaws of Life, hand drills, riot shields, medical beds |
| uranium ore | 30 | shale, basalt | high-tier stock parts, KA mods |
| diamond ore | 50 | shale, basalt (rare; none in siderite) | advanced research, bluespace beakers, tier-4 parts |
| bluespace crystal | 50 | shale, basalt (rarest; none in siderite) | bluespace items, research |

**Gibtonite** is an explosive rock that looks like plain rock (the scanner shows it). When you hit it, the chat says
**"There's gibtonite inside! It's going to explode!"** and the tile's name changes to "gibtonite deposit". It explodes
after 8-10 pulses (4-5 seconds): heavy damage within 3 tiles, light within 5. **Hit it again and it explodes at
once**, so stop shooting. Either click it right away with a **mining scanner** (manual or automatic) to defuse it
("The chain reaction stopped!"), or run more than 6 tiles away. Leave defused gibtonite where it is: it's an
explosive, and the machines can't process it.

### 5. Ore vents (the most valuable thing you do)

An **ore vent** is a large mound in the rock: "small ore vent", "medium ore vent" or "large ore vent". The one already
working by the base is the "active ore vent". Vents nearest the base are **small**, the middle third are medium and
the farthest are large. `search {query: "ore vent"}` whenever you enter a new cave. When a vent is near, the
automatic scanner pings.

1. **Scan it**: hold a mining scanner (manual or automatic) and click the vent. Stand still for 4 seconds until "vent
   scanned!". You get **100 mining points**, the vent gets a GPS signal, and examining it now lists its ores ("This
   vent produces small boulders containing ..."). Scanning every vent you find is quick, safe income.
2. **Prepare**: clear yourself room, be at full health with the KA charged, and keep **away from lava**. If an ash
   storm is due, wait for it to pass.
3. **Start the excavation**: click the vent with the scanner again. A tgui alert asks "Are you ready to excavate this
   ore vent?". Answer Yes. The vent clears the rock within 5 tiles around it, and a **NODE drone** lands on it. The
   drone has 400 HP on a small vent, 500 on medium, 600 on large.
4. **Defend the drone**: fauna spawn 3-4 tiles out in **waves** ("wave 1/5"). A new wave comes only after the last one
   is dead. Small vents: 5 waves of 1 monster. Medium: 10 waves of up to 2. Large: 15 waves of up to 3. The monsters
   are goliaths, legions, watchers, chasm lobstrosities, brimdemons and bileworms. Stand near the vent so they go for
   you, kill whatever attacks the drone first, and back off between KA shots. If the drone dies or is knocked off the
   vent, the vent closes ("the mining attempt fails"). You can retry later. **Your life comes before the drone.**
5. **Win**: "vent tapped!". Everyone within 7 tiles with an ID gets **600 points** for a small vent, 1100 for medium,
   1600 for large. From then on the vent sends boulders to the station's BRM forever. Radio it on Supply: "Tapped a
   small vent: silver, gold, plasma."

**Never excavate a "menacing ore vent".** It summons a megafauna. And leave boulders on vent tiles alone: the BRM
collects them.

### 6. Bringing ore home: the ore redemption machine

1. Pull the ore box onto the shuttle, fly home, and pull it through the dock and the Mining Office into the Cargo Bay.
2. **Pull the ore box onto the ORM's intake tile** (the Cargo Bay tile just west of the machine): it empties into the
   machine. With only a satchel, stand on the intake tile and use the satchel in hand: the ore it dumps goes in.
3. Open the ORM: first click the windoor in front of it ("Cargo Security Window" from the Cargo Bay side, "Ore
   Redemption Window" from the lobby), then click the ORM. The window ("OreRedemptionMachine") shows:
   - **Unclaimed points**: `tgui_act Claim` puts them on your ID (worn is fine).
   - **materials**: the silo's contents. `Release {material_ref: <row id>, sheets: N}` (at most 50) puts sheets out
     into the Cargo Lobby, for someone without a lathe.
   - **alloys**: `Smelt {path: <row id>, sheets: N}` makes Plasteel (1 iron + 1 plasma), Plastitanium, Plasma Glass,
     Reinforced Plasma Glass, Titanium Glass or Plastitanium Glass from silo stock.
4. **Points from the boulder machines** collect inside them. Examine one: "N mining points stored". Hold your **ID
   card** in your hand (take it from the ID slot), click the boulder refinery and then the boulder smelter, and answer
   the number box with the amount. Put the ID back.
5. Radio what went in ("Silo: +40 silver, +25 gold, +60 plasma") on Supply, and on Science for the Scientist.

### 7. Spending mining points

The mining equipment order console (tgui "ProduceConsole") shows your `points` and a catalogue. Add items to the cart
(`add_one {target: <item ref>}` or `cart_set {target, amt}`), then choose:
- **Purchase**: 65% of the list price (the price the window shows). The order arrives on the next cargo shuttle as a
  locked mining crate, which the Quartermaster or a cargo technician brings you. It needs a cart worth at least 200
  at list price.
- **Express**: full list price. A drop pod lands **on your tile** about 3 seconds later. It's harmless, and it works
  at the Lavaland base consoles too.

| Item (as the console names it) | List price | Why |
|---|---|---|
| survival emergency medipen | 250 | instant brute and burn healing on Lavaland; buy several |
| brute trauma treatment kit / burn treatment kit | 400 / 500 | proper medkits |
| bluespace shelter capsule | 350 | a spare shelter |
| wormhole jaunter | 650 | use it in hand to open a wormhole to a random teleport beacon on the station: your escape button. Worn on the belt, it saves you from a chasm fall. |
| Kheiral cuffs | 675 | worn in the gloves slot or on the jumpsuit: your suit sensors reach the station's crew monitor from Lavaland |
| mining weather radio | 320 | sounds a storm warning; on Supply |
| ventpointer | 1150 | points to the nearest untapped vent |
| advanced automatic mining scanner | 675 | 7-tile scan every 3.5 s |
| range increase / damage increase / cooldown decrease (KA modkits) | 675 each | a stronger KA (install by clicking the KA with the kit) |
| proto-kinetic crusher | 650 | melee alternative (see Defending yourself) |
| explorer's webbing | 450 | belt for mining gear |
| silver-plated pickaxe | 1000 | digs twice as fast as a pickaxe |

Explorer suits, GPS units and oxygen tanks are free at the base. Buy medipens first, then a jaunter, then cuffs.

## Lavaland survival

### Air, heat, lava, chasms

- **The air** is random each round: thin (30-49 kPa), between about 270 and 350 K, mostly oxygen, nitrogen and carbon
  dioxide in random amounts, sometimes with plasma, BZ or miasma. Most rounds that means too little oxygen and too
  much CO2 for your lungs. **Always wear the mask with internals on outside.** The low pressure itself doesn't hurt
  you (the warning icon is normal), and the explorer gas mask is fireproof.
- **Lava** (turf named "lava", drawn `~` in `look`): **20 burn damage per second, and it sets you on fire.** Never step
  on it, never dig into rock next to lava, and never fight near it, because a goliath grab or a lobstrosity knockdown
  can drop you in. On fire: get off the lava, press `B` (stop, drop and roll) or use the shower in the Production
  Wing's north airlock or an extinguisher.
- **Chasms** ("chasm", also `~`): rare. Falling in kills you and the body is gone. Walk around them.
- **Darkness**: the caves are dark. Wear the mesons, which show the shape of the rock in the dark, and carry the seclite.

### Ash storms

- They come every 5-12 minutes and last 1-2 minutes. 90% are real storms; the rest are harmless "emberfall" ("Gentle
  embers waft down around you...").
- **Warning, 30 seconds ahead** (to everyone on Lavaland): **"An eerie moan rises on the wind. Sheets of burning ash
  blacken the horizon. Seek shelter."** The base's barometric monitor turns yellow, then red.
- **The storm**: "Smoldering clouds of scorching ash billow down around you! Get inside!" **4 burn damage every few
  seconds to everyone outdoors**; the explorer suit doesn't stop it.
- **All clear**: "The shrieking wind whips away the last of the ash ... It should be safe to go outside now."
- **Shelter is anything indoors**: the base buildings, the shuttle, or a **bluespace shelter capsule**:
  1. Use it in hand: "... begins to shake. Stand back!", and your throw mode switches on.
  2. Click a clear floor tile 2-3 tiles away to throw it there.
  3. Five seconds later it becomes a 5x5 "Emergency Shelter" where it lies, pushing anyone out of the way. It needs a
     clear 5x5 (no rock, lava or dense objects), else "doesn't have room to deploy!": try elsewhere.
  4. Walk in through its glass door. Inside: air, a **stasis bed** (lie on it to stop wounds worsening while you
     wait for help), a GPS computer and 5 warm donk pockets in the fridge. It stays for the round.

### Fauna

All Lavaland fauna walk on lava unharmed. Your KA does **40 damage per shot** outdoors.

Fight fauna with the client's `fight` tool (crew basics §6): `fight {target: {"search": "goliath"}, goal: "kill"}` with
the KA in your active hand fires from range (3 tiles), steps back when it closes in, and steps off tentacles as they
come up. A goliath has 300 health: about 8 KA shots; call fight again if it answers before the goliath is dead.

| Creature | What it does | How to handle it |
|---|---|---|
| **goliath** | 300 HP, slow, hits for 25. **Tentacles burst from the ground** under or around you, grab you and hold you, with 10-15 brute to the legs. | Keep moving: never stand still near it. Shoot, step aside, shoot (8 shots). Butcher it with the knife (combat mode, click the corpse) for a **goliath hide plate**: click your explorer suit or hood with it for more armour, up to 3 each. |
| **legion** | 75 HP. Keeps its distance and sends flying skulls; one hit kills a skull. **The skulls turn anyone unconscious into a new legion.** | Rush it and kill the skulls. Never let yourself pass out near legions. It drops a **regenerative core** (see Healing). |
| **watcher** | 160 HP, floating eye, fires chilling blasts. When **"[watcher]'s eye glows ominously!"** appears, 1.6 s later it blinds and confuses everyone **facing it**. | At that line, step away from it, so you face away. 4 shots. |
| **chasm lobstrosity** | 150 HP. Charges to knock you down, then snips. | Step out of its line. 4 shots. |
| **brimdemon** | 250 HP. Fires a **beam straight ahead**: 25 burn, more while you stand in it. | Never stand in its row or column. 7 shots. |
| **bileworm** | 110 HP. Burrows, resurfaces, spits acid. | 3 shots. |
| **goldgrub** | Harmless; eats loose ore. KA shots bounce off. | Ignore it. |
| **raptor** | Neutral riding beast. | Leave it alone. |
| **necropolis tendril** | 800 HP, rooted in place. Lashes, grabs and throws spikes up to about 7 tiles; regenerates after 30 s untouched. In this version it doesn't spawn monsters or collapse into a chasm (the wiki is out of date). Its chest needs a skeleton key. | **Avoid it.** |

### Never approach

Megafauna show on your GPS by signal name, so you can keep away. Most have 1,300-2,500 HP; your KA does 40. If one
comes toward you, walk away at once. **Never lead one to the base or onto the shuttle.**

| Name | GPS signal | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ash drake** | "Fiery Signal" | flying dragon: fire breath, swoops that crush and gib |
| **bubblegum** | "Bloody Signal" | blood demon; charges |
| **colossus** | "Angelic Signal" | shoots volleys of bolts |
| **hierophant** | "Zealous Signal" | stays in the "Hierophant's Arena" ruin |
| **Legion** | "Echoing Signal" | inside the Necropolis, far north |
| **blood-drunk miner** | "Resonant Signal" | a dashing miner with a cleaver and KA |

Also never touch:
- a **"pulsing tumor"**: it summons a powerful elite creature;
- a **"menacing ore vent"**: it summons a megafauna;
- a **"necropolis tendril nest"** (a tendril among eggs): the **ash walkers'** home.

Ash walkers are lizard-like natives played by other players: spears, no need for air, hostile to trespassers.
**Leave them and their nest alone**; if they come at you, retreat to the base or the shuttle. Enter ruins ("Unexplored
Location") only healthy, with no storm due, after telling Supply where you are.

### Healing yourself

- **Survival emergency medipen**: hold it and click yourself. 8 epinephrine, 8 Libital (brute), 8 Aiuri (burn), 6
  leporazine (body temperature). **At low pressure (Lavaland outdoors) it injects at once; in normal air (indoors, the
  station) it takes 10 s and gives half.** Libital and Aiuri strain the liver: don't chain more than a few. The
  epinephrine medipen from your box stabilises you near crit.
- **Regenerative core** (a legion's drop): use it in hand **within about 4 minutes**, before it decays: 25 brute and 25
  burn healed at once, stuns cleared, a minute without injury slowdown.
- **Medkits**: on the Production Wing and EVA tables, two in the Emergency Medbay. **DeForest first aid stations** heal
  brute, burn and toxin slowly while your hand is in (a small charge to your bank account).
- **Badly hurt** (bleeding, broken bones, under half health): stop, fly home, `walk_to {area: "Medbay Lobby"}`, and
  radio Medical on the way.

### Getting found

- The station's **crew monitor doesn't show anyone on Lavaland** unless they wear **Kheiral cuffs**. Your **GPS** and
  your radio are how the crew finds you: every GPS lists all signals with coordinates, so give yours on Supply ("near
  the small ore vent, GPS 120, 80").
- A rescuer comes by the public lavaland shuttle or the mining shuttle and follows your GPS.

## Dangers and common mistakes

- Using the **Mining Office console before boarding**: the shuttle leaves without you. Board first.
- Forgetting the **BRM and conveyor**. They start off, and without them the tapped vents feed nobody.
- **Walking onto lava**, especially when `walk_to` plans over it (see Through the client). Look first.
- **Going outside without internals**, or with the gas mask still folded up.
- **Staying out when the storm warning comes.** You have 30 seconds: get indoors or deploy the capsule at once.
- **Shooting gibtonite twice.** After the gibtonite line, scanner or run.
- Starting a vent hurt, near lava or with a storm due; chasing attackers far from the drone.
- **Fighting indoors with the KA** (a quarter of its damage).
- Megafauna, pulsing tumors, menacing vents, the Necropolis, the ash walkers' nest.
- Hoarding ore instead of unloading every trip, and going silent on the radio.

## Emergencies

- **Evacuation called** (the emergency shuttle never goes to Lavaland): finish within a few minutes, get on the mining
  shuttle, fly home, unload ore on the way if there's time, then `walk_to {area: "Departure Lounge"}`.
- **Radio silence**: if your radio stops working, telecomms (or the base's relay) is down. Keep mining safely and talk
  in person when you're back.
- **Attack on the station**: you aren't Security; don't fly into a fight. If the Captain or Security calls for help,
  come back and act as crew-basics says.
- **Someone hurt on Lavaland**: get them indoors or into a shelter (stasis bed), use a medipen if they're dying, and
  call Medical with GPS coordinates.
- **You're dying out there**: medipen, then shelter, then radio Medical ("Shaft Miner down at Lavaland, GPS ...").
  A **wormhole jaunter** gets you to the station.
- **Mass casualties on the station**: make sure the silo has silver and gold for defibrillators.

## Defending yourself

- **Proto-kinetic accelerator**: 40 brute, range 3 tiles, one shot every 1.6 s. It **does only a quarter (10) above 50
  kPa**: inside the base, on the shuttle and anywhere on the station. The pressure is checked where the shot hits, so
  shooting from outdoors into a room is weak too. It empties when you put it away; draw it about 2 s before you need
  it.
- **Proto-kinetic crusher** (Crusher Kit or 650 points): two-handed (use it in hand to wield it, with your other hand
  empty). It does 20 brute wielded. Right-click a creature at range to fire a destabilising mark. The mark works only
  on **large creatures** (most fauna, never people), lasts 30 s and is ready after 0.8 s. The next melee hit detonates
  it for +50, or +80 from behind. In this version it has no pressure penalty, so it's a solid melee weapon on the
  station too.
- **Survival knife** (15), **pickaxe** (15), **compact pickaxe** (10): ordinary melee. The explorer suit's armour
  (melee 30) helps against people too.
- **When to stand and fight**: single ordinary fauna in the open, at full health and away from lava, shooting and
  stepping back. **Retreat**: from groups, a brimdemon lining up, anything when you're under half health, megafauna
  always, and other players you can't beat.
- **On the station** you fight like any crew member (crew-basics). Your KA is weak there; the knife or pickaxe are your
  real weapons. Your personal file says how readily you fight.

## Working with the crew

- **Quartermaster** (your head; cargo technicians report to them too): tell them when you leave, what you tapped and
  what went into the silo, and ask what's short. They deliver your shuttle-ordered crates, may want surplus gold,
  diamonds, uranium, silver or plasma to sell (release the sheets at the ORM), and can put the silo on hold. If no
  Quartermaster is on shift, report on Supply anyway and take requests from the Head of Personnel or the Captain.
- **Scientist**, on **Science (`:n`)**: your main customer: silver, gold, titanium, uranium, plasma, later diamond and
  bluespace, all through the silo. Ask them to research **Mining Technology** and **Low-Pressure Excavation** (KA
  modkits at the cargo techfab).
- **Medical Doctors**: defibrillators need silver and gold; tell them when it's in. Also who you call when hurt.
- **Station Engineers**: plasma for generator fuel, plasteel (smelt it at the ORM), titanium for Jaws of Life; release
  sheets if they want a stack in hand.
- **Chemist**: may ask for silver or a few sheets of plasma or uranium: release them into the Cargo Lobby.
- **Captain and other heads**: gold or a diamond on request; everything else belongs in the silo.
- **Other shaft miners**: work in pairs. One draws the waves at a vent while the other shoots, and everyone near it
  gets the full points. Split ore runs; leave other miners' lockers alone.
- **Bitrunners** share your office (the Bitrunning Den). Leave their machines alone.
- **Security** may send prisoners to the Labor Camp. Never free them.
- On **Supply**, keep lines short: leaving, arrived, vent tapped, sheltering, returning, unloaded.

## Through the client

- **One map at a time**: each `load_map` replaces the last.
  - In the Lavaland Production Wing:
    `load_map {path: "/workspaces/seeker-mcp/testbed/tgstation/_maps/map_files/Mining/Lavaland.dmm"}` (one z-level;
    default z). Its warning that the game's map is "MetaStation" is expected there; ignore it. Once you've seen about
    20 tiles of the base, `walk_to {area: "Mining Station EVA"}` (or "Mining Station Emergency Medbay", "Mining Station
    Public Lounge") works.
  - Back on the station, load `/workspaces/seeker-mcp/testbed/tgstation/_maps/map_files/MetaStation/MetaStation.dmm`
    again before any `walk_to {area: ...}` there.
- **Lavaland's map file holds only the fixed parts**: the base, the Labor Camp, the Necropolis and some lava and basalt.
  The caves, lava rivers, ruins and vents are generated at round start. Tiles you haven't seen are **planned as open
  floor**, so walks run into rock and replan. Dig through with the KA, or use `explore` for openings.
- **`walk_to` never walks over lava or a chasm you have seen**, unless a catwalk covers it (the base's bridges are
  catwalks, so walks cross them). Lava not seen yet is planned as floor, like any unseen tile: near lava, `look`
  first (`~` is lava, liquid or chasm). `move` doesn't check: never `move` onto `~`.
- **Dig with `walk_to {x, y, bump: true}`**: with a pickaxe or drill in your active hand, the walk plans straight
  through rock and digs each rock tile it walks into (it waits there until the tile gives way), so one call tunnels
  through several walls to the tile you name. The answer's `bumped_through` lists what it dug and `bump_with` the
  tool. To mine a vein, walk `bump` to a point inside or past it and walk over the ore it drops; to clear an area,
  walk `bump` back and forth through it. It refuses without a mining tool in hand (swap hands with `X`), never digs
  very strong rock or walls, and stops at once if a rock turns out to be gibtonite (get 5+ tiles away). Prefer it to
  clicking rock one tile at a time; the KA is still faster at range for a single tile.
- **GPS coordinates are the x,y numbers `walk_to` takes** (same z).
- **Firing the KA**: hold it in the active hand and `click` the target tile or creature, at most 3 tiles away. Then
  `wait {seconds: 2}` before the next shot. A click before it recharges does nothing.
- **Tgui alerts and number boxes**:
  - The vent's "Begin defending ore vent?" alert: `tgui_act choose {"choice": "Yes"}`.
  - The boulder machines' point claim: `tgui_act submit {"entry": N}`.
  - The GPS `rename`: `submit {"entry": "<name>"}`.
- **Radial menus** (voucher kits, suit storage unit): the answer lists the menu buttons; click the one you want by id
  while it's open.
- **Right-click** the BRM (`click {object_id, right: true}`) to toggle automatic retrieval. A plain click on the
  conveyor switch toggles the belt.
- **Use in hand** means clicking the item in your active hand: the scanner (pulse, or switch on the automatic one), the
  satchel (dump), the capsule (arm it), the gas mask (unfold), the GPS (window), the crusher (wield).
- **Ore on the scanner**: right after a pulse, `search {query: "mining overlay"}` may list the rock tiles holding ore
  for 3.5 s (unverified: the highlight is a short visual effect). Otherwise dig toward the veins near vents.
- **Storm lines** arrive as chat: when you `wait`, give `until: "eerie moan"`.
- **Ore box**: `ctrl` click to pull, `key H` to let go before doors. Cycling airlocks: wait 5-10 s, walk in again.

## Checklist

Shift start:
1. Suit sensors to tracking. Get to the Mining Office.
2. Locker: KA, mesons (worn), shelter capsule, manual scanner, compact pickaxe.
3. Right-click the BRM to automatic. Click the conveyor switch on.
4. Redeem the voucher (Survival Capsule and Explorer's Webbing, or Crusher Kit).
5. Supply: leaving. Science: ask what's needed.
6. Board the shuttle, then use the shuttle's own console: `move` to `mining_away`.
7. At the base: `load_map` Lavaland.dmm. In EVA: explorer suit and hood, explorer gas mask, full oxygen tank in suit
   storage, GPS renamed, automatic scanner on.

Ongoing loop (about 20-30 minutes per trip):
1. Before going out: internals on, KA charged, health full. Check the barometric monitor.
2. Scan every vent you pass (+100). Tap small vents near the base first.
3. Mine ore veins with the KA, pulling an ore box. Leave gibtonite alone. Stay off lava.
4. Storm warning: indoors or capsule within 30 s.
5. Hurt: medipen, then retreat. Below half health: home to Medbay.
6. Box full, or every 30 minutes: fly home, intake tile, Claim. Claim the boulder machines' points with your ID in hand.
   Radio what went in.
7. Spend points on medipens, a jaunter and cuffs. Re-check the BRM is still on.
8. Evacuation called: come home and go to the Departure Lounge.
