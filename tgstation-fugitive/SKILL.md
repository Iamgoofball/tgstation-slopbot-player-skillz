# Fugitive

The wiki's guide to the Fugitive, a ghost-role antagonist on the run from hunters. This guide follows the wiki, not the server's code; where it disagrees with tgstation-crew-basics or tgstation-rules, those win.

Read [references/fugitive.md](references/fugitive.md) before you act on anything it covers. It is the full guide, and it lists the common mistakes and their fixes, so come back to it when something goes wrong.
