# Traitor (tgstation)

Taken from the /tg/station wiki's Traitor page, not checked against the server's code. `crew-basics.md` and `server-rules.md` win where they disagree.

## 1. The role, and the rules

You spawn in your normal job, secretly working for the Syndicate (or, sometimes, on your own agenda). You have objectives and an uplink. Completing the objectives earns **greentext** at round end; failing costs nothing. You may ignore objectives for a better scheme.

**Rules:**
- You may kill, sabotage and destroy to get your objectives done. Rule 4 frees a lone antagonist from most rules; the limits that still apply (`server-rules.md`, section 4) are: no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC or IC in OOC, and no spawn-camping arrivals.
- Rushing to end the round annoys other players; other traitors may not want you in their way.
- **Blood brothers are a team antagonist:** never harm your brothers or their work.
- If you're the AI and a traitor, you are a malfunctioning AI instead (separate guide).

## 2. The uplink

- Starts with **20 telecrystals (TC)**. A **threat level** also gates items, so big items (like the syndicate bomb) can't be bought in the first minutes.
- Location, one of:
  - **PDA:** in the messenger app, set the ringtone to your code (a phonetic letter plus three digits). Lock it after use.
  - **Headset:** tune to the given frequency.
  - **Pen:** twist the head to two given angles, like a safe.
  - **Uplink implant:** pre-installed; costs 4 of your starting TC.
- The uplink also holds your objectives.
- Check the **discounts** every round.
- Don't spend everything at once: a 6 TC doorjack is wasted if you then find the Captain's spare.
- TC can be taken out as physical crystals and handed to another traitor, for items over 20 TC.

Combos the wiki suggests:

| Combo | TC | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Energy sword + energy crossbow | 18 | Down with the bow, finish with the sword. |
| Chameleon kit + agent ID + doorjack (airlock override) + storage implant + syndicate encryption key | 20 | Disguise, all channels, any door, a hidden pocket. |
| Binary translator key + cryptographic sequencer (emag) + hacked AI upload module | 20 | Emag a cyborg, subvert the AI, talk to silicons. |

## 3. Variants

- **Code words:** every traitor gets code phrases and responses; any spoken one is highlighted for all traitors. Work them into normal sentences (order a drink by that name on Common). Responding marks you as a traitor to them. Solo traitors owe each other nothing and may betray each other.
- **Blood brothers:** a flash converts one person to your team (maybe they get a flash for a third). Don't flash people in sunglasses or anyone who may have a mindshield. No TC. Share access across departments. Teams may be each other's targets: check a dead suspect's PDA messages for brothers.
- **Double agents:** currently disabled.
- **Contractor:** spend all starting TC on the Contractor Kit (baton, tablet, stealth gear, trinkets). Bring contract targets, dead or alive, to the given location for extraction and TC; alive pays more. Targets come back later, so the crew will learn there's a contractor.

## 4. Stealth and access

- Your access is your job's, plus any ID you take. See the wiki's Illicit Access page for break-in methods.
- Wear a spare ID (an assistant's) over your own for dirty work. A mask shows the ID's name, but your voice gives you away without a voice changer. Fake moustaches hide you like a gas mask or helmet.
- A **wallet** (dorms, or leather from the biogenerator) holds 4 IDs.
- Cameras see 7 tiles, like you: if you can't see one, it can't see you. X-ray cameras (curved bulge) see through walls. Screwdriver + wirecutters disable a camera; the AI may notice.
- The AI can't examine what you hold, but can photograph it.
- Putting things in or taking from a bag shows a message to everyone near; box-sized items only to adjacent people.
- Latex gloves leave partial prints; black gloves resist fire; insulated are best. Soap removes prints.
- Nobody checks locker-room lockers.
- A pAI (dorms, bar, library) can open doors quietly.
- Know maintenance; build hidden rooms with false walls.

## 5. Bodies and getting caught

- Revival needs a mostly intact recent body (defib), a blood sample (replica pod) or a brain (borging; borgs usually rat you out). **Remove the brain or the head** to stop almost all of them. Toolbox tools work; kitchen knife, botany hatchet as substitutes. Ditch the body fast: it bleeds.
- Body bags draw fewer questions; look like you're heading to medbay.
- Hide bodies: Toxins test site, far side of the AI Satellite, station exterior nooks, hidden maintenance rooms, EVA suit storage units, locked uncommon-access lockers. **Strip the jumpsuit or turn off sensors** first. Space them at an angle off the usual lines. Disposals moves them away.
- Wear gloves.
- **Caught:** lie consistently, agree to searches that won't find anything, shift blame, have a story for what you're carrying. Any visible traitor item ends the bluff: once you're exposed, the crew will try to kill you, so have an escape ready.

## 6. Tricks from the wiki

- Plasma-injected power cells or bulbs explode on use.
- Thrown glass shards may sharpen (4 → 15 damage).
- Disposals routed to space kill whoever goes in. You can uncuff yourself inside a disposal unit.
- Hand teleporter + tracking beacon in space.
- Banana peels and soap.
- Chased: double back past them; close doors behind you, open the ones you don't take; you can't open a door mid-close.
- With HoP access, close open Security job slots.
- A cigar dipped in plasma. Mousetrap + grenade in a bag. Reverse-pickpocketing a minibomb.
- Bombing or deconstructing the engineering SMESes frees the engine.
- Bullets pass windows but not grilles; lasers and disablers pass both; electrodes neither. Click a window to shoot it.
- Spraying fuel or alcohol on someone smoking or holding a lit welder/esword ignites them quietly.
- Canisters can be broken open by force.
- EMP disables headsets and messes up APCs.
- Circle a mech with an axe; it turns slowly.
- Lockers don't protect from vacuum.
- Wirecutters steal a flash from a wall flasher.
- Cargo bounties buy goodie cases (.38, energy guns); an express supply console skips the weapon-permit check.
- A suspicious toolbox does 15 instead of 10.

**Emags:** doorjacking a welded/bolted door seals it for good. Emagged cyborgs serve you and get tools (stun arm for engineering/mining, toxins for medical, lube for janitor, and so on). Emagged bots: poison medibots, floor-ripping floorbots, acid cleanbots, arrest-everyone secbots, lethal ED-209. Emagged arcade: Outbomb Cuban Pete wins a bomb, loss kills you.

**Other:** chameleon projector dodges shots and lets you drift in space. C4 timer goes up to 60000 s. PDAs hold a pen slot for a sleepy pen or dagger. Steal-the-blueprints: a photo counts. Steal-the-disk: take the pinpointer too. To force the round to end: delaminate or sabotage the supermatter, bomb enough of the station, EMP near the AI (15% chance of a shuttle call), or pump deadly gas into the air supply from Atmospherics. Reinforced tables in the escape-shuttle airlocks keep people off it.

## 7. Gimmicks and admins

- Gimmicks are encouraged: hitman, arms dealer, crime boss, a serial killer who leaves clues for the Detective. Objectives aren't the ceiling.
- **Pray** (`*pray`, or the Pray verb) to ask admins for help with a gimmick, offering TC for boons. They owe you nothing; be polite and original. If your objectives are done or boring, pray for more. Emagging a command console lets you message the Syndicate (also reaches admins).

## 8. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Uplink left unlocked | Lock it after each use. |
| Spending all TC at once | Buy when you need it; check discounts. |
| Traitor item seen | Keep items hidden; storage implant; don't stuff bags in view. |
| Body found with sensors on | Strip the jumpsuit or turn sensors off. |
| Fingerprints everywhere | Gloves; soap. |
| Talking while disguised | Your voice is yours; use a voice changer or stay silent. |
| Flashing a mindshielded or sunglassed person (brother) | Check first. |
| Hurting your blood brother | Never; team antag rules. |
| Ending the round in the first minutes | Work your objectives first; chaos is fine, a rushed round annoys everyone. |

## 9. For the crew

- You need reasonable cause before you go after a suspected traitor (rule 4). Once you are certain someone is an antagonist, lethal force is fine and expected: most can't be held safely, and a dead antagonist goes back into the ghost pool for midround roles. Confirm first (a traitor item, an uplink, being caught in the act), then don't bother with non-lethal arrest.
- Tells: someone carrying things they shouldn't have, avoiding searches, a mask and an ID that don't match their voice, cameras going dark, bodies with sensors off.

## 10. Through the client

- Uplink in the PDA: `click` the PDA (in hand) to open its window, `tgui_window` to read it, `tgui_act` to set the ringtone to your code and to buy. Check the window's actions before guessing names.
- Code words: `read_chat` and watch for highlighted lines; answer with a `say` that includes a response phrase.
- Blood brother: flash with the flash in hand, `click` the target.
- Pray: look for the Pray verb with `verbs`, run it with `call_verb`.
- Body work: `inventory` and the strip menu (see `tgstation-security-officer` for `tgui_act` keys).

## 11. Checklist

- [ ] Objectives and uplink code read; code written down.
- [ ] Discounts checked; TC kept for when needed.
- [ ] Spare ID or disguise before acting; gloves on.
- [ ] Plan for each objective, with gear bought to match.
- [ ] Uplink locked.
- [ ] Escape: on the shuttle or a pod at round end if that's an objective.
