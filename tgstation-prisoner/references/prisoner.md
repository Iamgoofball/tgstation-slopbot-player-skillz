# Prisoner (tgstation)

Rewritten from the wiki's Prisoner page. It is not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md`, those win.

## 1. The job

**You are not an antagonist.** Rule 1 (and every other rule) binds you as it does any crew member. You spawn in the permabrig with up to two other prisoners and no backstory. You're listed under Security but you aren't Security. Superiors: the Warden and Security. Access: none. Difficulty: easy; good for new players and veterans alike.

The game's policy text for prisoners, which admins enforce:

- You are not an antagonist; you chose this role. To walk the halls again, **earn** freedom: escape, or work out release terms with Security or a Lawyer. You may be useful manpower against threats.
- If you escape, **minimise harm** and preserve your own life, and accept the consequences if caught again.
- Otherwise, enjoy the wing.

**Bare minimum:** don't wordlessly stab the Warden when they bring in a new prisoner. **Basic:** roleplay, make a backstory, talk to your cellmates. **Advanced:** create some conflict and keep Security on its toes, within the rules.

## 2. The permabrig

A small garden, a basketball hoop, a laundry machine, toilets and showers, some food, a microwave and a vat of prison slop, an arcade machine, two card decks and a bag of dice. A holopad (the AI can talk through it). The materials for a spear and a jailbreak are in there, unnamed by the page.

## 3. Things to do

- Cards, dice, the arcade (a tiny chance at a pulse rifle prize), drawing, dyeing clothes.
- Grow plants in the garden. The biogenerator can turn plants into biomass and monkeys (the page jokes about flooding the wing with them to force release; that would be griefing Security's brig, so don't).
- **Exercise:** working out improves fitness (better at stairs and punches). Sleep after (lights off, bed, blanket = better sleep, better gains). Prison soup, milk and pump-up help.
- Build a backstory and talk to cellmates; negotiate with the Warden or a Lawyer for release.

## 4. Escaping

Allowed, as long as you minimise harm. Mind:

- Resisting a valid arrest with violence is not allowed (`server-rules.md` section 5); slipping away without harm is.
- Don't kill or loot officers; don't detain them longer than needed to get away.
- **Don't break windows to space.** The page's joke (break the garden window, get dragged into the grille) is how you die, and spacing the wing endangers others.
- After escaping you're a fugitive, not an antagonist. Help the crew against real threats if you can.

## 5. Mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Acting like an antagonist | You're not one. Rule 1 applies. |
| Attacking the Warden at the door | Talk; ask for terms; escape only without harm. |
| Breaching the wing to space | Never. Find a quieter way out. |
| Sitting silent all round | Make a backstory, chat, play cards, work out. |

## 6. Through the client

- `look` the wing; `search` for the arcade, seeds, cards.
- Talk to cellmates with `say`; there's no radio, so call for the Warden through the intercom (`:i` near one) if there is one, or ask the AI at the holopad.
- Exercise: `click` gym equipment; sleep with `command "Sleep"` or the rest verb (`verbs` to find it).

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Backstory made.
- [ ] Cellmates greeted.
- [ ] Something to do: garden, cards, workout.
- [ ] Release talks with the Warden or Lawyer, or a harmless escape plan.
