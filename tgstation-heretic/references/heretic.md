# Heretic (tgstation)

The wiki's main Heretic page. It is not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md` (the `tgstation-rules` skill), **those win** on the few limits in section 11. The page sends you to two sub-pages, "Heretic/Paths" and "Heretic/Conjurations", for each path's spells, robes, rituals and ascension; **those weren't provided**, so path details are missing here (section 11).

## 1. The role in one paragraph

You are a crew member (you keep your job, ID and access) secretly seeking forbidden knowledge. Your goal is to **Ascend**: research enough knowledge and sacrifice enough targets, then perform your path's ascension ritual, which is announced to the whole station. Knowledge points buy spells and rituals on a research tree. You start weak; most of your power comes from what you research.

## 2. Getting knowledge points

| Source | Points |
|---|---|
| Draining an influence by hand (10 s) | 1 |
| Draining with the Codex Cicatrix (twice as fast) / Codex Morbus (4x as fast) | 2 — but **visibly marks you** as a Heretic while you do it |
| Sacrificing a target | 2, or 3 for a Head of Staff |
| Sacrificing a Blood Cultist (target or not) | 1, plus one of three special rewards |
| Passive | 1 every 20 minutes |
| Ritual of Knowledge | 4 (once per round per the page's wording) |
| Feast of Owls ritual | 5, but **locks your ascension** forever |

If another Heretic sacrifices you, they siphon 3 points from you.

## 3. Influences

- They spawn at random around the station; only Heretics see them while untapped.
- **Right-click** one with an empty hand to drain it (10 s, 1 point). Draining also partly refills Mansus Grasp.
- Each can be harvested **once**. Afterwards it vanishes, then returns as a glowing "pierced reality" that everyone can see (anyone may fish from it). Don't bother with ones already taken.

## 4. Sacrifices

- At round start you get **five targets**: one Head of Staff, one Security member, one from your own department, two random. Only targets count. If you have none, stand on a transmutation rune and invoke **Heartbeat of the Mansus** to get some.
- Track them with the **Living Heart** (you start with one; if lost, transmute a poppy and a pool of blood to awaken a new one).
- Bring the target down (Mansus Grasp, blade, any weapon). In **critical, asleep or dead** they're ready. Drag them onto a transmutation rune and invoke Heartbeat of the Mansus.
- The victim is healed and sent to the Shadow Realm to survive; they return **with no memory of who sacrificed them**. Several organs drop on the floor (keep them for rituals) and are replaced with corrupted ones.
- A past victim who attacks you, or whom you attack, vomits up a chest organ (short cooldown between); 50/50 that it drops and kills them.

## 5. Transmutation rune

- Needs a writing tool (pen, crayon). With **Mansus Grasp active in your off-hand**, click the floor to draw: a green rune, 3x3. Keep it clear of clutter.
- Remove a rune by using Mansus Grasp on it. You may use other Heretics' runes. The Codexes draw and remove runes faster.
- **To transmute**: put the ingredients on the rune and click it with an empty hand.

## 6. Innate knowledge (known from the start)

| Knowledge | Effect |
|---|---|
| Break of Dawn | **Mansus Grasp** (8 charges): touch knocks down and deals heavy stamina damage, and marks the victim; hitting the mark with your blade detonates a path effect. |
| Heartbeat of the Mansus | Ritual to sacrifice a target in crit or worse, or to get targets if you have none. |
| The Living Heart | Tracks targets (see 4). |
| Feast of Owls | Ritual: +5 points, ascension locked, can't be undone. |

## 7. The research tree

- **Pick a path first**: open your **Antag Info UI** (action button, top left), choose a Path, then click that path's Eldritch Blade to learn node 1. Nothing else can be researched before that.
- Research in the Antag Info UI, **Research** tab (third).
- **Main tree**: 12 nodes, from path knowledge (1) to Ascension (12). Required to progress.
- **Knowledge Shop** (side knowledge): 5 tiers, unlocked by main nodes 2, 4, 6, 7 and 10. Optional: fighting, capturing, utility.
- Free drafted side knowledge offered at nodes 3, 5, 8 and 11.
- **Passive ability**: level 1 on choosing a path; level 2 when you craft your robes (also unlocks the Ritual of Knowledge); level 3 on ascending or doing the Ritual of Knowledge.
- **Spell charges**: researching gives a full spell; each cast uses a charge. Refill with an **Amber Focus** worn (every 3 minutes), your path's conditions, or dedicated rituals. Mansus Grasp also refills from influences and sacrifices. **Holy water drains charges every 3 s**: vomit it out.

## 8. Blade and robes

- **Sickly Blade**: your first research. Transmute **any knife** (a shiv works) plus a path ingredient. 20 brute, can cause bleeding.
- **Break the blade** (use it in hand) to teleport to a random, mostly safe spot. Lost at the point of no return.
- Blade limit: Ash, Cosmos, Lock, Rust, Void 2; Flesh 3; Blade path 4. Removed at the point of no return.
- **Robes**: from the "Armorer's Ritual" node; transmute path-specific materials. Each path's robes differ.
- Other conjurations (potions, books, amulets, thralls) are on the missing Conjurations page; some work for non-heretics too.

## 9. The point of no return

Crafting your path robes **or** reaching 10 knowledge points permanently: removes blade breaking, removes the blade limit, and gives you a **visible aura** that marks you as a Heretic to everyone. Choosing **Feast of Owls** or the **Lock path** avoids the aura. Plan for being hunted openly after it.

## 10. Ascension

- Requirements: research **20–22** knowledge (you start with 6) and **sacrifice at least 5** crew. Flesh path also needs **2 summoned monsters** (ghouls don't count).
- Each path's ritual and world-ending effect is on the missing Paths page. Ascending is announced station-wide.

## 11. Rules and page gaps

- You are a lone antagonist (rule 4): kill, sabotage and use every destructive spell and ascension effect your path gives you. Once the aura shows, crew have cause to kill you on sight, so go loud.
- Rule 4 still limits you: no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC (or IC in OOC), no spawn-camping arrivals. Everything else that serves your objectives is fair game.
- **Expect to be killed once exposed.** Crew may kill an antagonist as soon as they're sure what it is; most antagonists can't be held safely, and a dead one goes back to the ghost pool. Stay hidden until you're ready to go loud, then fight to win.
- Page gaps: path spells, ingredients, robes, ascension rituals and conjurations aren't in this page. Read the Antag Info UI and each node's text in game.

## 12. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Not picking a path, so nothing researchable | Antag Info UI, pick a path, click its blade. |
| Draining with a Codex in public | It outs you; do it hidden or by hand. |
| Hitting 10 points without being ready | The aura comes automatically; get your robes and gear first. |
| Losing the Living Heart | Poppy + pool of blood on a rune. |
| Dragging a target around with no rune | Draw the rune first, somewhere private. |
| Drinking holy water | It drains charges; vomit it out. |
| Fighting past victims normally | They vomit an organ when you fight: use it. |

## 13. Fighting a Heretic (crew)

Once you're certain it's an antagonist, lethal force is normal and expected: don't try to arrest or brig it. Confirm first.

Look for the aura, glowing pierced realities, green 3x3 runes and people draining things. Holy water drains their spells. Sacrificed crew come back with no memory of their attacker and with corrupted organs: get them to medbay.

## 14. Through the client

- The Antag Info UI is an action button: `screen` lists it; `click` it, then read it with `tgui_window` and choose path or research with `tgui_act` on the actions the window shows. Don't guess action names: read the window first.
- Drain influences with an empty hand: `click` the influence with `right: true`.
- Draw a rune: pen in one hand, Mansus Grasp active in the off-hand, `click` the floor. Transmute: ingredients on the rune, empty hand, `click` the rune.
- Sacrifice: pull the victim (Ctrl-click, `click` with `ctrl: true`) onto the rune, then invoke on the rune.
- Break the blade: hold it, `key {key: "Z"}` (use in hand).
- Your objectives: `read_chat` and the notes verb (`verbs`, then `call_verb`).
