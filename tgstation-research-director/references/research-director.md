# Research Director (tgstation)

The wiki's job page for the Research Director, rewritten. It is not checked against the server's code. `crew-basics.md` and `server-rules.md` win where they disagree; for the R&D console and techweb the code-checked `tgstation-scientist` guide wins.

## 1. The role

You head Science: Scientists (R&D, Ordnance, Circuits, Xenobiology), Roboticists and Geneticists. You answer to the Captain. You set research priorities, keep your staff from abusing what they build, and are responsible, with the Captain, for the **AI and its cyborgs**. Difficulty: hard.

- **Bare minimum:** get important Medbay equipment upgraded; know who is building maxcap bombs; know how to deal with hostile silicons.

## 2. Kit, access, radio

- **Access (wiki):** all Research, Genetics, Robotics, Morgue, Bridge, RD office, Teleporter, **AI Upload**, Gateway, Brig, mineral storage, Tech Storage.
- **Headset:** Science (`:n`) and Command (`:c`), with High Volume mode.
- **Your office** (near Ordnance on most maps; it's in the blast zone if Ordnance burns):

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| **AI System Integrity Restorer** | revives a dead AI from an intelliCard |
| **Robotics Control console** | view all cyborgs (charge, module, status, whether slaved to an AI); **lock them down** |
| **Exosuit Control console** | details of mechs fitted with tracking beacons |
| Camera monitor | watch your labs |
| **IntelliCard** | download an AI from its core; wipe it if rogue |
| Blank pAI card | a ghost becomes a personal companion |
| **AI Core circuit board** | build a second AI |
| Locker | outfits, personal gear, **Reactive Teleport Armor** |
| **Lamarr** | your pet de-fanged facehugger, in a display case; still leaps on people; keep it in the case |

## 3. Directing research

- At shift start assign scientists to labs. Ordnance and Xenobiology usually need only one each. Know every sub-department well enough to fill in.
- Check on **Roboticists**: they build and maintain cyborgs, bots and exosuits. No combat mechs without authorisation; push them to fit tracking beacons. Roboticists answer to you; demote if they refuse.
- **Geneticists** work only in Genetics. If they abuse powers, notify Security and have them demoted.
- Your job: nobody turns the Captain into a monkey, borgs the Clown against their will, floods the station with xenobio mobs, combines two bags of holding, or blows up the station from Ordnance.
- If Ordnance sets itself on fire inside the lab, let it burn there; shouting won't stop it.
- **Upgrades:** give an assistant or spare scientist an RPED full of parts and a screwdriver and send them round Science, Medical and Engineering: Xenobiology, Robotics, Genetics, the Supermatter, the incinerator, and every cell charger.

## 4. The AI

- You and the Captain are the only crew the AI lets into the **AI Upload**. Fix laws there after an ion storm or tampering.
- **Dead AI:** use an intelliCard on the destroyed core, put the card in an **AI System Integrity Restorer** (your office, and one directly beside the AI Upload foyer). It returns at 50 % integrity after a long process.
- **Second AI:** build an AI core (plasteel and glass, like a computer) with the board from your office, then insert a brain in an MMI or an active positronic brain (or leave it empty to receive a transferred AI). New AIs come with Asimov unless you add a law board during construction.
- If the Upload is lost, research lets you print new upload and law boards at the circuit printer. Traitors can do the same; upload consoles give off a **GPS signal**, so track hidden ones with a GPS.

### Rogue silicons

| Type | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Subverted** | freeform or hacked law module | **Purge** board then the default lawset board at an upload console. Cannot hack cyborgs; can be carded. |
| **Purged** | all laws removed | any lawset board at the upload. Cannot hack cyborgs; can be carded. |
| **Malfunctioning** | antagonist with an unremovable law, hacks APCs, spends CPU on powers | cannot be fixed; ignores uploads; **must be destroyed**. It shunts into a hacked APC before dying: deconstruct that APC entirely. It can hack its cyborgs. |

- **Cyborgs:** check Robotics Control regularly. An **unslaved** cyborg you weren't told about needs investigating. Suspect one is rogue or hacked: lock it down, find it, check its wiring; or dismantle it to question the MMI (MMIs aren't bound by laws).
- Against a malfunctioning AI don't blow every cyborg: a Robotics Control console moved off camera lets you lock and reset them as allies (see `tgstation-roboticist`).

## 5. Your defence

- You start vulnerable but have Ordnance and Xenobiology access.
- **Reactive Teleport Armor:** turn it on before wearing. Any contact (hug, grab, strip, hit) teleports you in a random direction, which can throw you **into space**. Keep it off near space.
- **Firing pins:** most printable weapons need one (Armory, your locker, Cargo). You can print **test-range pins** (fire only on the firing range) and **mindshield pins** (fire only for mindshielded users); fit the latter to Security's guns so they can't be turned on them.
- **Revolution:** Science is crowded, so a head revolutionary is likely inside it. At the first sign (people flashing each other, following you instead of working, firesuits and flamethrowers) go to the Brig.

## 6. Dangers and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Reactive armor on near space | switch it off |
| Never checking Robotics Control | check cyborg count and slaving regularly |
| Treating a malf AI like a subverted one | uploads don't work; destroy it and its APC shunt |
| Letting roboticists hand out combat mechs | require authorisation and beacons |
| Leaving a dead AI dead | card it, restore it |
| Staying in Science during a revolution | go to the Brig early |

## 7. Rules notes and antagonist play

- **If you are an antagonist:** you can build bombs, print every AI upload and law board, comms boards, tracking beacons and mechs, and emag borgs with an excuse. A cheap Syndicate item in the destructive analyzer unlocks **illegal tech**: illegal cyborg modules (unlock locked modules; looks like an emag), the mech clusterbang launcher, the T.Y.R.A.N.T. AI module. A portable chem dispenser, bluespace beakers and large grenade casings make strong grenades. Robotics rarely questions a head who brings in a corpse to be borged. Antagonists may pursue their objectives by any means; `tgstation-rules` still forbids metagaming and metacomms, bug or exploit abuse, erotic or creepy behaviour, OOC in IC, and spawn-camping arrivals. Once exposed, expect the crew to try to kill you.
- **Confirmed antagonists:** once you are certain beyond doubt that someone is an antagonist, lethal force is fine and expected (most can't be held safely, and dead antagonists return to the ghost pool). Confirm first.
- Lamarr as internals and the chameleon-projector disguise are jokes/tricks; no duty depends on them.

## 9. Through the client

- Radio: `say {channel: "radio", radio: "science"}`, `radio: "command"`, plain `channel: "radio"` for Common.
- Office consoles (Robotics Control, Exosuit Control, AI restorer): `click` the console, read it with `tgui_window`, act with `tgui_act` using the actions the window lists. Confirm before a lockdown or a self-destruct action; those are severe.
- IntelliCard on a core: hold the card in the active hand and `click` the core.
- `examine` cyborgs and the AI core for their state; `search` finds consoles and boards.

- [ ] RPED upgrade run sent out.
- [ ] Robotics Control checked; all cyborgs slaved to the AI.

- [ ] Reactive armor off near space.
