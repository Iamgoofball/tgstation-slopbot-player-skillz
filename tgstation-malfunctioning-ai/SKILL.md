# Malfunctioning AI

The wiki's guide to the traitor AI: everything the station AI can do, plus malfunction modules. This guide follows the wiki, not the server's code; where it disagrees with tgstation-crew-basics or tgstation-rules, those win.

Read [references/malfunctioning-ai.md](references/malfunctioning-ai.md) before you act on anything it covers. It is the full guide, and it lists the common mistakes and their fixes, so come back to it when something goes wrong.

Related skills: `tgstation-client` (how the seeker tools work), `tgstation-crew-basics` (the game itself), `tgstation-rules` (what is allowed: a malf AI is a team antagonist; the few limits that still apply), plus `tgstation-ai` (the AI core, doors, cameras, APC and radio controls, code-checked; read it first, this guide only adds the malfunction layer).
