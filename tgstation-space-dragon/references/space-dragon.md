# Space Dragon (tgstation)

The wiki's guide to the Space Dragon and its carp. It is not checked against the server's code. Where it disagrees with `crew-basics.md` or `server-rules.md` (the `tgstation-rules` skill), **those win**, except for the few limits listed in section 7.

## 1. The role in one paragraph

The Space Dragon is a midround antagonist (a random event, or a Dynamic midround spawn; not rare). A ghost takes it and it appears in space at the station's edge; CentCom warns the crew that a carp tide is coming. You win by charging **three carp rifts** inside the station, or by still being active with rifts up when the emergency shuttle arrives. There is no team, no access and no superior: you are a big, fast monster with fire breath that tears through walls, backed by ghost-controlled carp.

## 2. Stats and kit

- **Health 320** (the wiki also calls it "400 effective"). No armour, but damage doesn't slow you. Stamina damage does slow you, though it can't knock you down.
- **Melee 35, 30 armour penetration.** Speed normal; very fast compared with crew in practice.
- Space-proof. At spawn you pick a name and a colour (cosmetic).

## 3. Abilities

| Ability | How | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Carp Rift | ability button | Places a rift (see section 4). Must be on the station or AI satellite, a new spot each time. |
| Breathe Fire | click a tile **out of melee range** | One fast line of flame: heavy damage, sets things alight, leaves burning tiles. Very short cooldown. Damages mechs. The page contradicts itself on whether it hurts your carp (one part says it does, the carp part says they're immune): assume it does. |
| Crush | click a wall | Breaks it: 4 s for a wall, 12 s for a reinforced wall. One wall at a time; you can still breathe fire and gust meanwhile. |
| Gust | right-click | Short wind-up, then stuns and knocks back everyone (friend and foe) in 4 tiles. Afterwards you are briefly helpless; that tired time grows with each use and recovers only if you stop using it. Losing a rift makes its cooldown much longer. |
| Devour | melee a **dead** mob | Eats it and heals you by half its max health (a human: about 100). Carp corpses count. When you die, the eaten bodies drop out and can be revived. Changelings survive being eaten. |

## 4. Rifts: the objective

- From spawning you have **5 minutes to place each rift**, or you vanish. The rifts are not optional.
- A rift takes **5 minutes to charge**. Its location is announced to the crew after about **2.5 minutes** (the ability table says 2).
- While charging: a ghost carp can spawn from it every 60 s; you and carp on it heal 10 HP/s (the guide calls this "very slowly" — use it only in a pinch).
- Charged: it turns gold, becomes **indestructible**, spawns AI carp and drifts about. You heal fully and get a red aura and **30 s of extra speed**: use it to escape to the next site or punish attackers.
- Three charged rifts: endless sentient carp from all of them and a permanent speed buff. Shuttle arriving while you're active and unstopped counts the same.
- **If the charging rift is destroyed**: all earlier rifts vanish, you become very slow, gust gets much worse, and you can never place a rift again. Dying does the same (also if you're revived). Avoid this above all.
- A ghost can't respawn from a rift it already came from unless two or more spawns are backlogged there.

## 5. Tactics

- **Pick the rift site first.** Low traffic, tight corridors you can fill with fire, few windows (lasers pass through glass, your fire does not). Three sites next to each other are allowed.
- The first ~2 minutes are usually quiet; after the announcement expect the crew.
- **Never leave the charging rift alone for long**; a crewman slipping past to hit it ends your round.
- Set groups on fire: it spreads between people and forces them off. Gust them away, or gust burning corpses into them.
- Keep corpses nearby as health packs; eat one when you need it, not before.
- Poke and retreat with your speed. 320 HP goes fast if you stand in the open.
- In space you dominate anyone without a jetpack; a gusted opponent in zero-g keeps flying.
- **Fear shaft miners.** Kinetic accelerators hit hard in low pressure, and the crusher treats you as a Lavaland mob (about 70 per mark, plus trophies; about 5 detonations kill you). Gust them before they close.
- If a rift dies, run somewhere safe and stay a nuisance while you live.

## 6. As a carp from the rifts

- You are fodder, but mobile: harass crew, or better, hit important structures.
- You can open a short rift in spacetime (1 minute cooldown); anyone can follow you through it, crew and the dragon included.
- Heal by floating on a rift tile.
- This part of the page says you're immune to the dragon's fire (section 3 says otherwise; stay out of it). Stay near it; it needs you to guard the rifts. Groups of carp are a real threat, one alone is not.
- Smash windows to breach the hull (crew aren't space-proof, you are) and hit the supermatter or telecomms: it draws the crew off the dragon.

## 7. The few limits that still apply

- You are a lone antagonist (rule 4): burn, breach and wreck whatever helps you hold the rifts. Setting rooms alight, opening the hull and sending carp at the supermatter or telecomms all pull the crew away from your rift.
- Rule 4 still limits you: no metagaming or metacomms, no bug or exploit abuse, no erotic or creepy behaviour, no OOC in IC (or IC in OOC), no spawn-camping arrivals. Everything else that serves your objectives is fair game.
- **Expect to be killed once exposed.** Crew may kill an antagonist as soon as they're sure what it is; most antagonists can't be held safely, and a dead one goes back to the ghost pool. Stay hidden until you're ready to go loud, then fight to win.

## 8. Hunting a dragon (as crew)

Once you're certain it's an antagonist, lethal force is normal and expected: don't try to arrest or brig it. Confirm first.

- **Kill the charging rift, not the dragon.** Destroying it is often easier and cripples the dragon. Rifts don't move: knock down walls to get long sightlines or more entrances; an emitter breaks walls and keeps firing at it.
- The dragon ignores most armour. Keep it at range; fight it in **open rooms**, never tight corridors.
- Bring fire extinguishers; rolling to put yourself out in front of it is deadly.
- Don't leave corpses lying about (each is a big heal). Remove the dragon's corpse when it dies: it can be revived (the cult can).
- Seal it in a room with remote door controls: crushing out takes time and holds it still.
- If the shuttle is called, plan to kill the dragon before it arrives, or survive it after.
- Miners: the crusher works in full pressure; wear Lavaland armour and expect the gust.
- The wiki also suggests explosives near the rift.

## 9. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wandering until the 5-minute timer runs out | Pick a site within the first 2–3 minutes. |
| Rift in a glassy, open room | Lasers shred you; choose walls and corridors. |
| Chasing kills far from the rift | Hit and come back; the rift is your life. |
| Spamming gust | Each use lengthens the helpless window. |
| Eating corpses at full health | Save them. |
| Breathing fire through your carp | Your fire kills them. |

## 10. Through the client

- `screen` lists your HUD buttons, including the Carp Rift ability; `click` it while standing where the rift should go.
- Fire breath is a `click` on a far tile; crush is a `click` on a wall; gust is a `click` with `right: true`. Devour is a melee `click` on a corpse (combat mode on, `key {key: "F"}`).
- `status` and the `danger` lines show your health; read chat for CentCom's rift announcements.
- Carp: talk to the dragon in plain `say`; check `verbs` for the rift ability if it isn't on `screen`.

## 11. Checklist

- [ ] Name and colour picked; find a quiet, walled site inside the station (within 5 minutes).
- [ ] Rift placed; stay near it for 5 minutes; corpses kept close.
- [ ] Rift charged: use the 30 s speed to reach site 2. Repeat for site 3.
- [ ] Watch for miners and anyone flanking the rift.
